# Opening — No one names the distributor

Na and Lim question students caught gambling.

They warn that another offense may result in juvenile detention.

Most say they learned about the games from friends and cannot identify the original distributor.

Na says there is no realistic way to map the entire network in one day and prepares to continue the following morning.

Then Lim notices a different crime nearby.

# Boys break into a car

Several youths teach one another how to identify an unlocked vehicle.

They say a folded or unfolded side mirror can indicate whether the doors were secured.

They learned the method from webtoons and news reports.

They find an open vehicle and steal an iPad.

One boy says selling the devices will cover the interest they owe.

Another says they should not repay anything and should target something bigger.

Na and Lim catch them.

---

# Gambling debt

Lim checks their phones.

The boys insist that they can simply return the stolen goods.

Na asks about the “interest” they mentioned.

They admit that recruiters approached them near an internet café and demonstrated a gambling win.

They watched **50,000 won** become approximately **1.6 million won** and began thinking that studying, university, and employment were pointless compared with immediate profit.

Within days they lost their savings, sold a computer, and had nothing left.

The recruiters returned with loans.

Small amounts became hundreds, and the demanded repayment grew into several million won.

The collectors are too frightening to resist.

One boy says a collector is among the top three fighters at **Guwon High Tech** and is supposedly the best student there.

Na recognizes the connection.

# Kwon-hyuk disappears

The agency checks Jang Kwon-hyuk’s workplace.

His supervisor says he suddenly called in sick for several days.

The timing matters: the previous day was payday.

Lim checks the school.

Lee Ho-sung and Jo Hong-gi are also absent.

Kwon-hyuk did not report to work, and the others did not attend school.

Na realizes that his friendly greeting alerted the group.

> “I MADE A HUGE MISTAKE.”

Kwon-hyuk noticed the investigation and hid.
