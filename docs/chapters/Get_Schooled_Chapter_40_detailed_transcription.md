## Strip 003 — Terms of the one-on-one

Park Eun-ha and Kang Soo-yeon face each other on the gym floor in yellow-and-blue uniforms. Teammates gather behind the sideline.

Lim Han-rim raises one hand and announces:

> “WHOEVER SCORES 5 POINTS FIRST WINS.”

Soo-yeon looks toward Lim.

> “KEEP YOUR PROMISE.”

> “IF I WIN, ARE YOU GOING TO GO BACK TO THE EDUCATION DEPARTMENT?”

Lim’s open-handed posture indicates she accepts the condition. The game is therefore not merely practice: Soo-yeon believes victory will remove Lim from the team.

# Chapter-level observations

### 1. The game forces objective comparison

Soo-yeon’s status had allowed her to define Eun-ha as weak. A first-to-five game removes the hierarchy and tests only skill.

### 2. Recruitment became captivity

Soo-yeon’s wish to keep Eun-ha “next to” her was not admiration. It was a strategy to prevent Eun-ha from developing elsewhere as a rival.

### 3. The 4–4 score destabilizes Soo-yeon’s identity

She is a national-team player and public “basketball queen.” Being taken to match point by Eun-ha proves that the threat she imagined two years earlier has become real.

### 4. The chapter delays the result to expose motive

Rather than end with a winning shot, the chapter stops inside Soo-yeon’s memory. The decisive issue is no longer who scores next, but why Soo-yeon spent years crushing Eun-ha’s confidence.
