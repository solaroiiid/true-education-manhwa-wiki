# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Mr. Go’s trust becomes exploitable because the school offers no procedural protection

His educational philosophy is not the direct cause of his death; institutional haste converts an accusation into social guilt.

## Yeri understands the asymmetry between students and teachers

She expects schools to investigate adults immediately while treating student misconduct cautiously.

## The staged cake weaponizes appearances

Yeri creates a scene where any rejection can be portrayed as hostility toward reconciliation.

## Hanrim’s approach deliberately rejects Mr. Go’s softness

The chapter does not yet prove her method is better; it establishes that she will not permit Yeri to control the narrative through tears and accusations.
