# Strips 002–003 — Lim tests the bully’s confidence

After the fiction disclaimer, Lim Han-rim has Hong Song-hak’s wrist and forearm locked in both hands. His face is bruised and bandaged from the previous chapter.

Song-hak reacts to her description of the earlier lesson as “easy.”

> “WHAT DID YOU SAY? EASY…?”

Lim asks:

> “WHY? DID IT HURT YOUR FEELINGS?”

She says it because it is true and invites him to free his hand if he disagrees.

> “YOU’RE JUST A LITTLE BITCH…!”

The visual framing alternates between Song-hak’s strained body and Lim’s faint, confident smile.

Lim thinks that she considered how far she could go because, no matter how corrupt he is, Song-hak remains a minor. Looking at his behavior, however, she questions whether she needs to worry so much about restraint.

# Strips 004–006 — Second period begins on the rooftop

Lim orders everyone:

> “EVERYONE FOLLOW ME TO THE ROOFTOP.”

Song-hak and his followers laugh at the dramatic instruction. They assume that Lim’s earlier success depended on surprise and that she is not a genuine martial artist.

On the rooftop at night, Song-hak asks whether she is a bully or simply fearless.

Lim says she wants to finish quickly because she plans to go home and watch baseball.

She formally announces:

> “FROM NOW ON, THE SECOND PERIOD OF THE SPECIAL EDUCATION CLASS WILL BEGIN.”

The first period made the boys experience the same games they used on other students. The second period has a different requirement:

> “KNEELING DOWN AND APOLOGISING TO THE VICTIM YOU BULLIED.”

They must continue until Lee Sang-wook forgives them.

Lim warns that if Sang-wook refuses, the night may become very long.

# Strips 007–009 — Song-hak pressures Sang-wook to “forgive”

Song-hak immediately tries to control the outcome.

He tells Lim that there has been a misunderstanding and claims they came to reconcile rather than retaliate.

He leans over Sang-wook with a distorted smile.

> “RIGHT? WOOK-A.”

> “WE MADE UP EARLIER, RIGHT?”

> “YOU SAID YOU’D FORGIVE EVERYTHING, RIGHT?”

Sang-wook’s internal narration observes that even after the agency arrived, Song-hak has not changed. He briefly wonders whether Song-hak is behaving this way because the person supervising him is a woman and whether he himself must continue living under this fear.

Then he rejects the arrangement.

> “IF I DO NOTHING, THEN NOTHING WILL CHANGE!”

He cries openly and tells Song-hak:

> “I’VE NEVER FORGIVEN YOU!”

> “IF YOU WERE ME, WOULD YOU…?”

Song-hak’s expression turns flat. His fist tightens.

> “YEAH…?”

# Strips 010–012 — Lim intercepts the attack

Song-hak answers:

> “THEN DON’T, YOU BASTARD!”

He swings at Sang-wook.

Lim steps between them and blocks or redirects the strike with a kick and rapid body movement.

> “I SHOULD STEP IN NOW, RIGHT?”

Song-hak complains that both Lim and Sang-wook are a pain.

When he demands to know what she means by stepping in, Lim hits him hard enough to launch him across the rooftop.

He regains his footing and laughs.

> “WOAH~ YOU FLEW AWAY, HAHAHA.”

He boasts that she was wrong to assume he would not strike a woman. Other students may have been nervous because she came from special forces, but he claims she still has “the physique of a girl.”

He tears off his outer shirt and displays a heavily trained torso.

> “MY HOBBY IS WORKING OUT!”

> “I NEVER MISS A DAY.”

He notices that Lim appears angry because he can continue taking hits.

# Strips 013–015 — Na and Chey discuss Lim’s combat ability

Song-hak declares that being hit is worth it and that he can be punched all night.

Na bursts into laughter.

> “SHE? GETTING HURT?”

> “LIM HAN-RIM?? TO THOSE KIDS???”

# Strips 016–020 — Lim dismantles the physical advantage

Back on the rooftop, Song-hak’s confidence collapses.

Lim’s kick feels, in his words, as though he has been sliced with a knife. She attacks vital points and keeps him off balance.

The fight becomes increasingly one-sided. Song-hak and his followers attempt to surround her, but she turns their size, clothing, spacing, and the rooftop environment against them.

A narration-like phone conversation continues over the action:

- The physical difference between men and women is no obstacle to Lim.
- She uses vital points, weapons, and the environment to reverse that difference.
- Even a large opponent is no match for her.
- Na does not know where she learned all her martial arts, but he has also sparred with—or “given her a shot” himself.

Song-hak’s clothing tears during the exchange. Lim continues with a cold, focused expression while the boys scream and collapse around her.

# Chapter-level observations

## 1. Sang-wook’s refusal is the central victory

The important change is not Lim’s ability to defeat Song-hak. Sang-wook finally says that he never forgave him, even while Song-hak is physically close enough to retaliate.

## 2. Song-hak uses “reconciliation” as another threat

He does not apologize and wait for the victim’s answer. He leans over Sang-wook and tells him which answer to give.

## 3. Lim’s assignment exposes sexist overconfidence

Song-hak interprets her body as proof that she must be weaker, even after she has already controlled him.

## 4. The physical lesson mirrors the bullying dynamic

The boys once enjoyed striking people who could not resist. Lim places them in the same position while preserving Sang-wook’s ability to speak for himself.
