## Strips 001–002 — Sera accuses Giryong directly

Sera stands over the injured Jungtaek and confronts Kim Giryong.

She calls his method especially dirty and underhanded.

> **USUALLY YOU PRETEND TO BE QUIET, AND MAINTAIN A POKER FACE…**

> **BUT YOU HIDDENLY BEAT HIM UP BEHIND THE SCENES LIKE THIS, HUH…**

She says framing the victim as the bully is even worse than the extortion itself.

Giryong answers that Jungtaek tried to stab him and therefore deserved far more than a beating.

Sera rejects the excuse. If Giryong did not want an investigation, he should not have created the bullying situation in the first place.

Giryong calls her a crazy woman who appeared from nowhere and began spouting nonsense.

> **SAY THAT AGAIN, YOU FUCKING BITCH!**

He launches a trained kick or takedown.

Sera is struck hard enough to be driven aside, surprising her with the speed and technique.

Giryong assumes she is finished.

---

## Strips 003–004 — Sera reveals the recording

Sera rises, only slightly shaken.

> **GEEZ~ YOU GOT ME WHEN I DIDN’T EXPECT IT.**

She observes that Giryong is not an ordinary school bully. His movement suggests formal training, possibly kickboxing.

Giryong warns her not to talk about what happened.

Sera smiles and holds up a phone.

> **I MEAN, WE LIVE IN AN INFORMATION-DRIVEN DIGITAL SOCIETY~**

> **THE VIDEO QUALITY IS AMAZING, RIGHT?**

The recording contains Giryong’s own explanation of the false-report tactic, the extortion, and his response to Jungtaek’s knife.

Giryong initially sees another opportunity. Because the same video also shows Jungtaek trying to stab him, he thinks it can be sent to Jungtaek’s mother for money.

He proposes demanding hundreds—then thousands—of won or more in exchange for silence.

Sera is disgusted:

> **AS IF THE TIT-FOR-TAT REPORTING WASN’T ENOUGH,**

> **YOU EVEN WANT TO BANKRUPT HIS FAMILY, HUH?**

Giryong orders her to hand over the phone.

---

## Strips 005–006 — Giryong recognizes that Sera can fight

The fight resumes.

Giryong notices that Sera’s balance and reactions are far beyond those of an ordinary transfer student.

He asks whether she trained in martial arts.

Sera mocks the question and says her punches do not follow the neat form he expects from a trained fighter.

Her style is direct, physically overwhelming, and unconcerned with looking technical.

Giryong attempts to exploit her lower body and targets the leg.

The attack fails.

---

## Strips 007–008 — The “caveman family” explanation

Giryong’s own leg buckles after colliding with Sera.

He cannot understand why her bones feel like solid metal.

Sera jokes that her father was once asked by a doctor what he ate to have such unusually dense bones.

Her father’s drunken explanation was that their family is practically a tribe of cavemen who never fully evolved.

She launches into an intentionally ridiculous monologue:

- prehistoric humans had thicker and harder bones,
- weighed more,
- could jump farther,
- and chased large animals for days.

She claims everyone in her family could outperform Olympic athletes.

Giryong shouts that the story is nonsense.

Sera casually agrees that her father was drunk when he said it.

The joke fills time while she continues dismantling his movement and confidence.

---

## Strip 009 — The false injury is exposed

Sera attacks the arm or hand Giryong has kept wrapped in bandages.

He screams.

She points out that the supposed injured limb has been part of the evidence supporting his victim narrative.

> **YOU HAD BANDAGES WRAPPED AROUND YOUR LEFT HAND, RIGHT?**

> **BE GRATEFUL, YOU PUNK. NOW YOU CAN STOP THE DAMN ACT!**

The chapter visually suggests that the bandaging was exaggerated or staged, though the exact medical condition is not independently established.

Giryong tries to negotiate.

He proposes that Sera join him instead of continuing the fight.

When he gets money from Jungtaek’s mother, they can divide it:

> **HOW ABOUT 50-50?**

Because Sera filmed the evidence, he even offers her seventy percent.

The offer confirms that he sees every injury and record as a financial instrument.

---

## Strips 010–011 — Sera refuses the partnership

Sera responds with more violence.

She says thugs like Giryong always act fearless until someone stronger beats them, at which point their true nature appears.

Daniel arrives during the aftermath.

Sera calls him:

> **MR. NON-VIOLENCE**

Daniel tells her:

> **GREAT WORK.**

Sera says this is only the beginning. She wants Giryong subjected to repeated beatings for six days and seven nights until he crawls to the education office begging the committee to reverse its ruling.

Daniel stops her.

> **NO, ANY FURTHER VIOLENCE WON’T TEACH HIM ANYTHING.**

He says the next action should be left to him.

The closing narration promises a lesson that will remain:

> **DEEP INTO HIS BONES.**

The phrase connects Daniel’s planned psychological method with Sera’s joking obsession with skeletal strength.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Giryong treats evidence as currency

The phone recording does not make him remorseful. He immediately considers using the knife footage to extort Jungtaek’s family.

## His victim performance contains staged elements

The bandaged arm helped persuade the committee, but Sera’s confrontation suggests the presentation was manipulated.

## Sera’s strength is not the educational conclusion

She proves Giryong can be physically defeated, but Daniel argues that repeated pain will only teach submission to a stronger person.

## The recording changes the case

The agency now possesses direct evidence of extortion, manipulation, and Giryong’s own description of the counter-report strategy.

## Daniel prepares restorative reversal rather than ordinary punishment

The next chapter places Giryong inside the same disbelief and institutional helplessness he created for Jungtaek.
