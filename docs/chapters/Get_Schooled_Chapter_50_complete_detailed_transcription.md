# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The park day tests whether the father can experience parenting without control

Play, food, and attention create a connection that punishment never produced.

## Na recognizes the cost of removing a parent

Protection may still require separation, but the child’s attachment cannot be treated as irrational or irrelevant.

## The father shows the beginning of reflection

The photographs and ordinary outing affect him, though the chapter does not claim permanent reform.

## The mother’s arrival widens the case

Minseo’s abuse exists within a mutually violent and unstable household rather than one isolated bad parent.

## The ending deliberately undercuts a simple redemption

One meaningful day cannot repair years of violence or guarantee that either adult will change.
