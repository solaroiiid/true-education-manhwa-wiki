### Content note

The chapter contains teacher harassment, forced immersion in water, alcohol-related misconduct, threats, and physical violence.

## Images 001–003 — A vacation complicated by students

Hanrim spends a rare vacation at the beach with her friend **Yu Seoyeon**.

The trip is meant to compensate for leave Hanrim previously missed while training the agency’s new recruits.

Seoyeon works as a substitute teacher and is exhausted by the unstable position, but the women initially try to enjoy the seaside setting.

Nearby, a group of older teenagers or university-age youths surround a female teacher in the water.

They laugh while forcing her under, treating her distress as entertainment.

The woman gasps and struggles to breathe.

The students describe the event as play between friends.

Hanrim notices the contradiction between the teacher’s fear and the children’s cheerful performance.

## Images 004–006 — The hotel-room gathering crosses boundaries

Later, the group occupies a hotel or pension room with alcohol.

The female teacher protests that students should not be drinking and attempts to confiscate the bottles.

The youths complain that they spent money on the trip and that the teacher has no charisma or authority.

They speak as if a temporary teacher is a service worker whose role is to entertain them.

One student demands reimbursement or asks why they should listen after paying roughly thirty thousand won.

The woman repeatedly apologizes rather than asserting authority.

Her survival strategy is to keep everyone calm until the trip ends.

## Images 007–009 — Hanrim and Seoyeon go out at night

Hanrim and Seoyeon walk through a crowded nightlife district.

Seoyeon comments on attractive strangers and lightly teases Hanrim about her boss.

Hanrim says the dark-haired man she knows is her superior and reacts defensively when Seoyeon calls him handsome.

The playful conversation is interrupted when a red-haired, tattooed young man approaches.

He is **Lee Cheolyong**, age twenty, from Seoul.

He and his companions claim they are on a university field trip or group outing.

## Images 010–012 — Cheolyong provokes Hanrim

Cheolyong flirts aggressively with Hanrim and offers her alcohol.

She refuses.

He continues crowding her and treating the refusal as part of the game.

Hanrim deflects or smashes it into his face, leaving him bloodied and stunned.

The confrontation reveals that the young man who intimidated the teacher is also willing to use force against an unfamiliar woman.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The teacher participates in her own silencing

She apologizes for the students because she believes conflict could cost her employment. That response protects the abusers and makes outsiders misread the violence as ordinary play.

## “University student” is used as moral cover

The youths insist they are adults when challenged, yet rely on the teacher-student relationship when it benefits them and mock her lack of permanent status when she tries to exercise authority.

## Hanrim’s vacation ends the moment the employment hierarchy becomes visible

She is not investigating because a friend was insulted. She recognizes a pattern in which temporary teachers are expected to absorb violence to preserve contracts.
