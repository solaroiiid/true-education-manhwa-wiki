# Chapter-level observations

### 1. Ja-yeon is punished through the same public sphere she weaponized

The online audience that defended her turns against her. The result is not clean justice: she faces threats and mob humiliation, showing that viral punishment can reproduce abuse.

### 2. Soo-yeon’s “protection” is exploitation

Her apology script protects only Soo-yeon. Ja-yeon is ordered to sacrifice her career and accept criminal responsibility so the older sister can remain eligible.

### 3. Lim individualizes punishment

She recognizes that Ja-yeon’s weakness is dependence and public image, while Soo-yeon’s weakness is athletic pride. The one-on-one match is designed specifically for Soo-yeon.

### 4. Eun-ha’s selection is both practical and symbolic

Putting Eun-ha in the starting lineup restores her identity as an athlete, not merely a victim. It also forces Soo-yeon to confront the talent she tried to suppress.
