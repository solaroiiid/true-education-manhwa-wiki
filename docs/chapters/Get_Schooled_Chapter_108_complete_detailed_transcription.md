## A controlled arterial injury

The pastor scrambles away, clutching his neck and screaming that he will die. Blood spurts dramatically, but then slows.

> **AHHHHHK! I’M GONNA DIE!**
>
> **B-BLOOD!**
>
> **H-HUH?**
>
> **THE BLEEDING STOPPED?**

He realizes Han-rim did not fully sever the artery. She had placed the point precisely enough to control him without killing him.

Han-rim immediately attacks again. She twists his head, punches his face with a bright impact burst, and breaks or stamps his leg.

> **DON’T WORRY… SCUM LIKE YOU…**
>
> **WON’T GET TO GO SO EASILY!**

The pastor howls as bone cracks.

> **AHHHHK! M-MY KNEE!**

Han-rim explains the boundary the cult crossed.

> **THE COUNTRY MAY GIVE RELIGIOUS GROUPS A LOT OF FREEDOM,**
>
> **BUT HURTING KIDS IS CROSSING THE LINE.**
>
> **I ASSURE YOU, OUT OF ALL THE OPPONENTS I’VE FACED,**
>
> **YOU WILL SUFFER THE MOST.**

She picks up a long metal pole or broken implement.

> **THE REST OF YOU AS WELL!**

## Han-rim withdraws mercy from the adult believers

Han-rim had previously regarded the congregation as victims of manipulation. Watching parents beat their own daughter changes that judgment.

> **UNTIL NOW, I CONSIDERED YOU ALL AS VICTIMS.**
>
> **HOWEVER, BY LETTING YOURSELF BE FOOLED AND EVEN HURTING YOUR OWN BLOOD, YOU’RE NOT DIFFERENT FROM HIM…**
>
> **I WON’T SHOW YOU ANY MERCY!**

She launches into the adult crowd. The pole sweeps through bodies in broad arcs; fists and elbows follow. Blood and Korean impact lettering fill successive action panels. Adults are knocked backward, doubled over, and thrown into one another. Han-rim fights despite her torn shirt, open wounds, exhaustion, and blood loss.

One cultist screams about a “bone,” while others scatter. Han-rim remains upright in the center of the field with the pole in hand.

> **HEHE~ IT’S A WURM~**

> **HEHE~ THE WURM IS SO FUNNY~**

> **HAH… YOU UNEDUCATED FOOL. IT’S NOT “WURM,” IT’S WORM.**

> **DON’T JUST STAND AROUND! THAT’S THE CRIMSON WITCH!**
>
> **LORD’S NEW TESTAMENT CHAPTER 3, VERSE 37–39!**

The children mechanically begin repeating fragments:

> **LORD’S NEW TESTAMENT CHAPTER 3, VERSE 37–39…**
>
> **THE DEVIL WHO ESCAPED FROM HELL…**

They raise sickles and rush Han-rim.

She looks directly at them. The next impact sequence sends the children and their tools flying, but the art emphasizes disarming and scattering rather than maiming them: sickles spin through the air while small bodies land in piles or roll across the ground.

The pastor uses the chaos to flee down a lane between buildings.

## Hwa-jin arrives as the “miracle”

The pastor lies flattened and bleeding. In his delirium, he interprets the arrival as divine fulfillment.

> **IT’S A MIRACLE…**
>
> **HE IS RECORDED IN THE LORD’S NEW TESTAMENT…**
>
> **THE FIRST MESSIAH!**

The “messiah” is revealed to be Na Hwa-jin, standing over the scene in his black suit after arriving by motorcycle. He looks around at the bloodied pastor, injured cultists, scattered children, and exhausted Han-rim.

> **WHAT’S GOING ON?**

The chapter ends on Hwa-jin’s baffled expression: his rescue arrival has been absorbed into the pastor’s delusion rather than breaking it.
