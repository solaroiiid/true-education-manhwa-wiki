## Images 033–040 — The limits of the TRPA

The teacher begs Na for help.

Na shows his identification and explains that the Teachers’ Rights Protection Agency reports directly to the Minister of Education.

That gives it strong authority in public education.

Child abuse and domestic violence are handled by police and the Ministry of Health and Welfare.

Preschool administration may also fall outside the TRPA’s direct jurisdiction.

Na cannot lawfully take custody of Minseo merely because the danger is obvious.

The teacher says that, if they do nothing, she fears Minseo will die.

Na agrees.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The preschool teacher acts beyond ordinary job expectations

## Minseo’s silence is survival behavior

He protects food, avoids answering adults, and runs because previous adult attention has led to punishment.

## Jurisdiction creates a dangerous gap

Everyone may recognize the abuse while each organization possesses only part of the authority required to stop it.

## Na begins as a reporting citizen

The legal limitation does not prevent him from observing, documenting, and remaining at the scene.
