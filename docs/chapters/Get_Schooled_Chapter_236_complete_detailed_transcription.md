The team cannot leave them hanging.

## Strip 002 — The door has been designed to trap them

Hanrim reaches the main door.

It will not open.

The entrance has been altered so it cannot be unlocked normally from the inside.

Na says the construction itself is part of the murder plan.

Smoke thickens rapidly.

The captives begin coughing and losing consciousness.

The chapter cuts to Ji Yun-gyeol, Park Ma-ri, and Kim Tae-yang watching the fire from a nearby building.

They raise glasses.

> **GOODBYE, AGENCY.**

Ma-ri says roughly ten minutes have passed.

That is more than enough time for carbon-monoxide poisoning to begin incapacitating the people inside.

They expect the apartment to become a sealed cremation chamber.

## Strip 003 — Yun-gyeol planned an accidental fire

Yun-gyeol describes the trap’s safeguards.

The windows are reinforced and cannot be broken easily.

Ventilation routes have been blocked.

Fire trucks have not yet arrived.

The remaining evidence can be arranged to resemble a battery explosion and accidental residential fire.

Then the flames on the surveillance feed begin diminishing.

Ma-ri wonders whether the sprinklers activated.

Yun-gyeol says he personally ensured that every sprinkler was disabled.

The video reveals Na and the inspectors using fire extinguishers inside.

Yun-gyeol is confused.

He removed the extinguishers from the apartment beforehand.

The team must have brought its own emergency equipment.

## Strip 004 — Emergency gear creates an escape route

Inside, Na and Hanrim continue trying the entrance.

The door will not move.

The white-haired technical officer opens a bag of equipment he brought as a contingency.

The kit includes tools capable of dismantling or cutting the modified door, including a power drill.

He says he never imagined the team would need the gear to escape a burning penthouse, but it was prepared in case forced entry became necessary.

The others hold cloth over their mouths and move the unconscious captives farther from the smoke.

The officer removes the door hardware while Na and Hanrim create room around him.

Once the lock and reinforced fittings are broken, the team forces the entrance open.

Portable extinguishers keep the flames from spreading to the floor below.

> **THE FIRE IS OUT.**

At minimum, the immediate path to the corridor is clear.

## Strip 005 — Yun-gyeol accepts the partial success

Firefighters and ambulances arrive.

Na orders Daniel and Sera to accompany the injured branch chiefs to the hospital.

Hanrim remains behind to explain the situation to the fire department.

Na says there is something else he needs to verify.

Across the street, Yun-gyeol’s group receives an assessment.

Investigators found that the penthouse entrance had been deliberately dismantled by agency equipment.

Ma-ri complains that they should have used a more decisive bomb.

Yun-gyeol disagrees.

Killing an entire Ministry agency inside a residential building would create uncontrollable political consequences.

G.Y. could be destroyed even if the inspectors died.

He says they can deal with the agency later.

For now, eliminating the rival branch heads is enough.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The technical equipment validates the agency’s preparation

The team survives not through luck alone, but because it carried tools and extinguishers for emergencies beyond the expected arrest.

## Yun-gyeol plans the official explanation as carefully as the fire

The battery-explosion story is meant to transform murder into an ordinary residential accident.

## He avoids total escalation for strategic reasons

Yun-gyeol is willing to kill inspectors but recognizes that openly destroying a government agency could mobilize the entire state against G.Y.

## Na locates the observers through personality and surveillance

He combines nearby CCTV with the assumption that the planners would want to watch their own trap.

## Tae-yang’s strength does not protect him from precision

Na does not compete in a prolonged power contest; he strikes a critical anatomical point and ends the first exchange.
