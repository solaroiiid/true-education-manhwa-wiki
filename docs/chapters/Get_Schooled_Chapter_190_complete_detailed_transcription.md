## Strips 001–003 — Minseo is hidden before the interrogation

Na listens to Minseo’s warning.

The boy says leaving immediately will place him in danger.

A woman hiding nearby watches the exchange through a cracked door or window.

Na crouches and speaks gently to the child.

> **MINSEO, WILL YOU WAIT INSIDE FOR A BIT?**

Minseo nods.

Na walks him into an inner room and says:

> **NO MATTER WHAT HAPPENS, DO NOT COME OUT.**

Once the door closes, Na’s expression changes.

He returns to the injured blue-haired fighter—**Seo Changho**, a senior member of G.Y.’s action faction.

> **NOW, ANSWER MY QUESTIONS.**

Na shows his agency identification and asks:

> **WHY ARE YOU GUYS TARGETING ME?**

Changho hesitates.

He says it is connected to a woman named **Choi Gayeon** and to the imprisoned G.Y. boss’s personal grievance.

The exact relationship is tangled by the scanlation, but Changho clearly believes Na interfered in a matter the boss regarded as ownership over a woman.

## Strips 004–005 — Na forces Changho to speak

Changho reaches toward a knife or concealed weapon rather than answering.

Na stamps or crushes his hand against the floor.

The fighter screams.

Na says he may not possess Hanrim’s frightening talent for extracting answers, but he interrogated prisoners during military operations.

> **MAKING SOMEONE LIKE YOU TALK IS A PIECE OF CAKE.**

Changho tries to laugh off the pain.

Na applies another joint lock and asks again.

The gangster finally gives a broader explanation.

G.Y.’s imprisoned boss issued a succession order:

> **WHOEVER BRINGS YOUR HEAD GETS TO TAKE HIS PLACE.**

The command transformed Na into a prize.

Different branches and executives now compete to kill him because success could make one of them the organization’s next leader.

Changho says the bounty resembles the agency’s recent challenge crisis, but the internal G.Y. reward is older and more serious.

---

## Strip 006 — The factions waiting behind Changho

A flashback shows numerous G.Y. members gathered around liquor, weapons, and branch leaders.

Changho explains that some factions still obey the imprisoned boss absolutely.

Others merely want his title, businesses, and control over the national network.

He planned to remain calm, wait for an opening, and seize the top position after killing Na.

Mari and other planners prefer indirect methods, while Changho’s action squad believes direct violence proves leadership.

Na asks for names and locations.

Changho initially promises to tell him later, then tries to delay.

Na reminds him that the police are already coming.

---

## Strips 007–008 — Two options

Police sirens approach the villa.

Na tells Changho:

> **YOU’VE GOT TWO OPTIONS.**

One option is to give the agency the names of the participating branches and executives.

The other is to call them all to the villa so Na can deal with them together.

Changho refuses.

> **I’M NO FUCKING SNITCH…**

Na grabs him and says the organization’s loyalty has already collapsed. Each faction is using the others as disposable shields.

Police enter the building.

Minseo is found and protected.

Officers identify Changho as a major suspect in the hostage-taking and organized assault.

A frightened witness points toward him.

Changho begins bargaining when he realizes the boss will not rescue him.

Na gives the police the information necessary to begin tracing the branch structure.

---

## Strip 009 — The case reaches the news

Television reports describe a police operation at a G.Y.-linked villa.

The authorities confirm that victims were rescued and multiple suspects arrested.

Another report notes that the incident is not formally treated as part of the public `WRECKTRPA` challenge. The crimes involve organized assault, hostage-taking, weapons, and gang activity rather than a simple online stunt.

The case is transferred to a public-security or organized-crime prosecution unit.

Na identifies himself to the detectives and hands over Changho.

An investigator thanks him for cooperating.

Na asks them to treat the matter as a serious criminal case even though many participants are young.

## Strips 010–011 — Changho’s statements lose credibility

During prosecution, Changho attempts to save himself through shifting stories.

He exaggerates, retracts statements, and accuses others whenever the evidence becomes inconvenient.

The prosecutor concludes that many of his unsupported claims are unreliable.

He is detained and held while awaiting trial.

A news segment shows his arrest and the collapse of his immediate operation.

Inside prison, G.Y.’s long-haired boss watches the report.

He is furious that one of his most aggressive subordinates failed.

> **NA HWAJIN…?!**

The man grips the television or bars.

A guard interrupts:

> **YOU’VE GOT A VISITOR.**

The chapter ends as Na arrives to speak directly with the imprisoned founder whose succession order placed the agency under attack.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Minseo knows the danger is organizational

The child is not merely afraid of Changho. He expects retaliation from other G.Y. members if he is seen leaving before police control the villa.

## The bounty produces factional competition

The imprisoned boss promises leadership to whoever kills Na, encouraging executives to attack without coordinating.

## Changho’s direct strategy contrasts with Mari’s

Changho relies on trained fighters, hostages, and weapons. Mari later uses information, online crowds, and public manipulation.

## Na offers cooperation before punishment

Changho can help dismantle the organization or remain the disposable member arrested at the failed villa.

## The prison visit moves the conflict to its source

Capturing Changho does not cancel the order. Na must confront the man whose authority continues beyond prison walls.
