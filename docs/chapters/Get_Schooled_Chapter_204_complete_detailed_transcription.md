## Strips 001–002 — Seoyoung cannot explain why Yunju was targeted

In the rain, Yunju’s mother repeats the question she has carried for eight years:

> **WHY DID YOU DO THAT TO MY LITTLE YUNJU?**

> **WHY THE HELL DID YOU HURT MY DAUGHTER?!**

She holds the red container Seoyoung brought for the planned attack.

Seoyoung believes the mother may pour its contents onto her and desperately searches for an answer that will save her.

She considers kneeling, begging, or inventing a justification.

Then the truth surfaces:

> **I DON’T KNOW…**

> **IT HAPPENED SO LONG AGO.**

> **I DON’T EVEN REMEMBER WHY I DID IT…**

The answer is horrifying precisely because Yunju’s death defined her mother’s entire life while the perpetrator cannot recall the original motive.

The confrontation becomes physical.

The exact mechanics are stylized and partly obscured, but the container is knocked or redirected during the struggle. The dangerous chemical Seoyoung brought splashes back onto her face.

She screams.

## Strips 003–005 — Seoyoung awakens with severe burns

Seoyoung wakes in a hospital bed.

Her entire head and face are wrapped in bandages.

The skin beneath them burns and itches.

> **WHAT HAPPENED TO MY FACE…?**

Daniel sits nearby.

> **HOW DOES IT FEEL TO HAVE YOUR OWN ASSAULT COME BACK TO YOU?**

He says the injury is the result of her own conduct.

> **NONE OF THIS WOULD’VE HAPPENED IF YOU HADN’T BROUGHT THOSE DANGEROUS CHEMICALS IN THE FIRST PLACE.**

> **THIS IS YOUR OWN KARMA, SO CARRY IT FOR THE REST OF YOUR LIFE.**

Seoyoung tears at equipment and demands an explanation.

Daniel calls her selfish to the end.

The wounds, rage, fear, and frustration she now experiences are exactly what she intended to force onto Haeran.

She believed she could hurt other people while remaining exempt from reciprocal harm.

Detectives enter the room.

They identify themselves and place her under emergency arrest for:

- instigating the assault on Jang Haeran,
- and attempted murder connected to the planned chemical attack.

The hospital has cleared her for transfer.

Seoyoung cries that everyone is being cruel.

## Strip 006 — “It’s all in the past”

Seoyoung repeats the defense she has used throughout the arc:

> **IT’S ALL IN THE PAST…**

> **IT’S ALL OVER NOW…**

She asks why people are going this far over something finished.

Daniel says that if she still refuses to learn, she should experience the consequences long enough for the lesson to become unavoidable.

The day after her arrest, Ok Foods begins collapsing.

Netizens investigate the company and uncover unrelated allegations involving:

- origin-labeling violations,
- exploitation of subcontractors,
- and the CEO’s sexual misconduct.

The outrage expands into a nationwide boycott.

The displayed share price falls sharply, including a daily decline of roughly twelve percent.

## Strip 007 — Her father severs ties; the accomplices face consequences

Ok Youngjun holds a press conference and publicly severs ties with his daughter as a minimal effort to protect Ok Foods.

He neither hires a lawyer for her nor visits her.

At approximately the same time, the scanlation labels **Lee Dabin** as a civil servant receiving an indictment based on past school-bullying allegations. This conflicts with the earlier workplace identification of Dabin as a hairdresser and Yuni as the civil servant; the contradiction is preserved rather than silently corrected.

The accused woman protests to Daniel:

> **THIS ISN’T WHAT WE AGREED ON! YOU SAID I’D AVOID PUNISHMENT!**

Daniel answers:

> **DID I SAY THAT?**

> **EVEN IF I DID, IS THERE A REASON I SHOULD KEEP THAT PROMISE?**

The exchange emphasizes that betraying an accomplice did not erase the witness’s own conduct.

Kim Yuni, indicted on the same allegations, stops leaving her home after Seoyoung’s arrest.

## Strip 008 — Haeran accepts the cost of public exposure

Narration says Yuni develops a lasting fear of people that may prevent her from returning to an ordinary life.

Haeran, meanwhile, is questioned not only as a victim but as a suspect.

She revealed the offenders’ real names and workplace addresses publicly.

Under Korea’s factual-defamation framework, true statements may still produce criminal liability when public interest and other defenses are disputed.

Haeran says:

> **I WAS PREPARED FOR THIS FROM THE START.**

> **I’LL ACCEPT WHATEVER PUNISHMENT COMES MY WAY.**

> **BUT I HAVE NO REGRETS AT ALL.**

Public petitions calling for abolition of factual defamation surpass one million signatures.

Seoyoung is placed in a solitary cell at the Seoul Detention Center as a pretrial detainee.

She immediately complains that there is no mirror.

## Strips 009–010 — Gratitude and the unanswered question

At Yunju’s memorial, her mother says the children who destroyed Yunju’s life are finally facing legal consequences.

She credits the TRPA officer standing beside them.

> **THANKS TO YOU, MY 8-YEAR BATTLE HAS FINALLY BORNE FRUIT.**

Haeran says she can finally begin releasing the wounds of the past.

Both women thank Daniel deeply.

Daniel watches them and asks a difficult question:

> **ARE YOU REALLY SATISFIED?**

> **DON’T YOU REGRET… WHAT YOU DID TO OK SEOYOUNG?**

The question refers to the moment in the rain when Yunju’s mother had the opportunity to direct the chemical deliberately and to the broader temptation to turn Seoyoung’s punishment into personal vengeance.

The chapter ends before the women answer.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Seoyoung’s answer reveals the scale of moral asymmetry

The mother has spent eight years demanding an explanation for an act Seoyoung no longer remembers committing.

## The chemical injury is a consequence of Seoyoung’s own weapon

The chapter does not present the burning as an unrelated attack. It results from the dangerous substance she brought to harm Haeran.

## Legal exposure spreads beyond the original bullying

The boycott uncovers corporate wrongdoing, while Haeran’s naming campaign creates a separate factual-defamation issue.

## Cooperation does not erase culpability

Providing evidence against Seoyoung may affect treatment, but it does not guarantee immunity for the accomplices.

## Daniel asks whether justice has become revenge

The arc has produced arrests, lost careers, damaged bodies, and corporate collapse. He wants the victims to examine which outcomes they actually sought.
