# Get Schooled — Chapter 109
## Complete Detailed Panel-by-Panel Transcription

### Scope
This transcript covers the 26 ordered images in Chapter 109, including scanlation support material. English wording is preserved as printed.

## Hwa-jin reaches the compound

The chapter opens with scanlation support information and the standard fiction disclaimer. The cult leader, still lying injured in the road, raises a hand toward the approaching Na Hwa-jin.

> **SHOW ME A MIRACLE…**
>
> **BY KILLING THAT WITCH…!**

Han-rim recognizes Hwa-jin immediately.

> **WHO ARE YOU?**
>
> **SUNBAE NIM… YOU’RE HERE?**
>
> **YOU CAME EARLIER THAN I EXPECTED…**

Hwa-jin explains how he found her. He contacted Seo Yeon from the emergency helpline and learned Han-rim had been investigating a cult that portrayed public schooling as evil.

> **I CONTACTED A FRIEND NAMED SEO YEON WHO WORKS ON THE EMERGENCY HELPLINE**
>
> **AND SHE TOLD ME YOU WERE LOOKING INTO A CULT THAT VILLAINIZED THE SCHOOL SYSTEM.**

He mildly reprimands Han-rim for entering a closed community alone.

> **I UNDERSTAND THAT YOU DON’T LIKE TO CONTACT ME WITHOUT BEING SURE WHETHER THERE’S A SITUATION…**
>
> **NO MATTER WHAT, IT IS DANGEROUS TO JUST CHARGE HEAD FIRST INTO A CLOSED SOCIETY.**

He surveys the unconscious adults and broken cult leader.

> **BUT I SEE YOU’VE MANAGED TO TAKE CARE OF THINGS YOURSELF.**
>
> **THIS GUY SEEMS LIKE THE LEADER, SO IS THIS MISSION COMPLETE?**

Han-rim hesitates.

> **NO, ABOUT THAT…**

---

## The children remain indoctrinated

The agents question Ju-hyeok and other children. Ju-hyeok cannot define exploitation and sees the greenhouse labor as sacred.

> **I’M NOT SURE WHAT THE WORD EXPLOITATION MEANS…**
>
> **BUT I’VE LEARNT THAT THE GREENHOUSE WORK IS HOLY WORK WHERE WE PLANT SEEDS.**
>
> **I DON’T NEED TO STUDY!**
>
> **ALL EVERYONE NEEDS TO DO IS LISTEN TO THE PASTOR!**

He calls harming the pastor the greatest possible sin.

> **I AM A HOLY WARRIOR. ANYONE WHO HARMS HIM, I’LL KILL THEM MYSELF!**

When asked whether he feels remorse for attacking his sister, Ju-hyeok rejects the relationship.

> **SHE’S NOT MY SISTER! SHE’S A DEVIL!**
>
> **BECAUSE THE PASTOR SAID SO! A DEVIL IS A DEVIL! ALL DEVILS MUST DIE!**
>
> **UNTIE ME! I’LL KILL YOU ALL TOO!**

Han-rim is visibly shaken by how thoroughly the children have been conditioned.

> **WHAT DO WE DO? THEY’VE ALL BEEN BRAINWASHED…**
>
> **EVEN IF WE PUT THE PASTOR IN JAIL, WE HAVE NO IDEA WHETHER THESE BRAINWASHED CHILDREN WILL BE ABLE TO LIVE NORMALLY AGAIN…**

Hwa-jin responds that children’s malleability works both ways.

> **CHILDREN ARE A PRECIOUS GIFT.**
>
> **IT’S EASIER TO BRAINWASH CHILDREN UNDER THE AGE OF 10 THAN TO BRAINWASH AN ADULT.**
>
> **THE SAME GOES FOR THE OPPOSITE.**
>
> **IF WE ACT QUICKLY AND PUT THEM UNDER PROFESSIONAL CARE, THEY WILL GET BETTER IN NO TIME.**

Han-rim feels relief, but Hwa-jin warns that saving the children is not the same as destroying the cult.

---

## Why arrest alone will not dissolve the organization

Adult believers kneel and beg the government officers not to take the pastor.

> **GOVERNMENT OFFICERS… PLEASE DON’T TAKE THE PASTOR AWAY…**
>
> **THERE IS NO GREATER PASTOR THAN HIM IN THIS WORLD.**
>
> **WITHOUT HIS GOSPEL, WE WON’T BE ABLE TO LIVE.**
>
> **YES… PLEASE TURN A BLIND EYE.**

Hwa-jin explains the aftereffects of pseudo-religion.

> **REGARDLESS OF WHETHER THE PASTOR IS JUDGED TO BE GUILTY OR NOT, THEY WON’T LISTEN TO A THIRD PARTY.**
>
> **LOCKING THE PASTOR UP WILL SIMPLY LEAD TO RESCUE ATTEMPTS.**

The cult also exists beyond the isolated compound. Even if the children are quarantined and treated, believing parents retain enormous legal and emotional authority over them.

> **YOU SAW DURING THE HELPER INCIDENT, RIGHT? JUST HOW MUCH AUTHORITY A PARENT HAS OVER THEIR CHILD.**
>
> **IF NOT ALL OF THEM, AT LEAST A FEW CHILDREN WILL FALL BACK UNDER THE INFLUENCE OF THE CULT.**

The leader boasts that imprisonment cannot end his power.

> **SATAN MUST HAVE GOTTEN IMPATIENT FOR HIM TO SEND SUCH A HIGH-CLASS DEVIL AFTER ME.**
>
> **KNOW THIS. EVEN IF YOU LOCK ME UP WE WILL…**

Hwa-jin interrupts and offers a bargain: the pastor must gather everyone the next morning and publicly dissolve the organization. In exchange, the agency will drop the charge of assaulting a civil servant.

> **TOMORROW MORNING, GATHER YOUR FOLLOWERS AND ANNOUNCE “THE CULT IS NO MORE~”**
>
> **IF YOU CAN DO THAT FOR ME, WE’LL DROP THE CIVIL SERVANT ASSAULT CHARGES.**

The pastor laughs. His followers are his strength and shield, and he believes officials cannot torture a civilian in a modern democracy.

> **CIVIL SERVANTS TORTURING A CIVILIAN IN THE 21ST CENTURY… THE WORLD WOULD GO CRAZY OVER IT… KEKEK…**

---

## Han-rim temporarily resigns

Hwa-jin agrees that a civil servant cannot do such a thing. Han-rim produces the resignation document that the cult prepared.

> **SUNBAE NIM, THE RESIGNATION.**
>
> **TEACHERS AGENCY SPECIAL OPERATIONS FIELD DIRECTOR IM HAN LIM.**
>
> **I OFFICIALLY TENDER MY RESIGNATION.**

Hwa-jin formally accepts it on the spot.

> **I ACCEPT YOUR RESIGNATION. GOOD WORK, IM HAN LIM.**

He then turns to the pastor.

> **SHE’S NOT AN AGENT ANYMORE, IM HAN LIM.**
>
> **SO WHATEVER HAPPENS BETWEEN CIVILIANS IS NONE OF MY BUSINESS. RIGHT?**

The pastor immediately collapses.

> **N-NO… HOLD ON…**
>
> **I… I’LL DO AS YOU SAY.**
>
> **I’LL DISSOLVE IT AS YOU ASKED…**

Han-rim refuses to stop. She wants repayment for the previous day and mockingly thanks him for providing the resignation letter that removed her official constraints.

> **DON’T HAVE TO DISSOLVE IT.**
>
> **I WANT TO KEEP ON PAYING YOU BACK FOR YESTERDAY.**
>
> **YOU SHOULD’VE SIGNED THE RESIGNATION LETTER AS YOU TOLD ME TO~**
>
> **HAVING NO OBLIGATIONS IS THE BEST~**

She applies military interrogation techniques that cause excruciating pain without leaving visible wounds. The pastor begs to be allowed to dissolve the cult.

> **AHHKK! PLEASE LET ME DISSOLVE…!**

Hwa-jin marvels at the technique. Narration explains that even resilient enemies once begged Han-rim to kill them; this is why her field codename is **Baba Yaga**.

---

## A staged revelation

The next morning, the pastor stands before the congregation seemingly unharmed. He thanks the saints for their prayers and claims their faith caused divine intervention.

> **COULD IT BE THAT YOUR PRAYERS AND MINE HAVE REACHED THE LORD?**
>
> **THE DEVILS WHO TERRORISED US YESTERDAY HAVE BEEN PURIFIED!**

He claims that while held in a windowless room, a ray of light appeared and God spoke to him.

> **ANGELS SHALL DESCEND, AND CLEANSE ALL EVIL.**
>
> **BEAR WITNESS, YOUNG LAMB, FOR ALL SIN SHALL BE CLEANSED FROM THIS WORLD.**

Han-rim watches from behind and quietly warns him to continue correctly.

> **GOOD, KEEP ON GOING. TRY ANYTHING FUNNY AND YOU KNOW WHAT WE’LL DO TO YOU.**

Hwa-jin explains that simply exposing the pastor as a con artist would fail. Followers would assume he had been threatened. They must work inside the cult’s own logic.

> **WE NEED TO STOOP TO THEIR LEVEL AND FOLLOW THEIR LOGIC TO TRULY DISPERSE THIS CULT.**

The pastor declares that evil has vanished, so the believers no longer need to remain in the confined “paradise.” He announces that they must leave for the wider world and that this is their final congregation.

---

## The dissolution fails

Some believers reject the announcement.

> **NO! YOU’RE WRONG!**
>
> **THERE’S NO WAY THE DEVIL’S INFLUENCE COULD HAVE BEEN DESTROYED OVERNIGHT!**
>
> **THIS IS ALL A CONSPIRACY TO SEPARATE US!**
>
> **THE DEVILS STANDING THERE HAVE POSSESSED THE PASTOR!**
>
> **LET US EXORCISE THE DEVILS AND CHASE THEM OUT!**

Han-rim wonders whether the pastor secretly signaled them. Hwa-jin realizes he misjudged the structure.

> **I MISJUDGED THE SITUATION.**
>
> **THE REASON BEHIND THIS PSEUDO-RELIGION LASTING SO LONG WASN’T BRAINWASHING OR A DOCTRINE.**
>
> **FOR THEM, RELIGION ISN’T AN OBJECT OF FAITH**
>
> **BUT A REFUGE.**

The chapter ends on that diagnosis. The congregation does not cling merely to a leader or theology; it clings to the community as an escape from ordinary life. Removing the pastor cannot remove the need the cult has been serving.
