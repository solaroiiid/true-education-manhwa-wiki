# Get Schooled — Chapter 1
## Complete Exceptionally Detailed Panel-by-Panel Transcription

### Scope and completeness

This document covers the **entire uploaded Chapter 1 MHT**: all **33 embedded vertical image strips**, arranged in their original reading order across **17 reader pages**. It transcribes every clearly readable English dialogue balloon, narration box, caption, credit, legal quotation, chart label, and prominent sound effect, while also describing the visual storytelling in detail.

No dialogue has been invented. Where the scanlation uses awkward grammar, unusual wording, broken words, or inconsistent punctuation, the printed wording is preserved and identified when useful. Visual interpretation is kept separate from literal transcription.

### Content note

This chapter contains severe school bullying, physical assault, institutional neglect, blood, discussion of suicide, and an interrupted suicide attempt.

### Transcription conventions

- **NARRATION:** Rectangular narration or internal-monologue box.
- **DIALOGUE:** Speech balloon or clearly spoken line.
- **SFX:** Visible sound effect. Korean effects are described by their likely sound or function when exact romanization is unnecessary.
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** Signs, law text, charts, cards, credits, labels, or other printed material inside the artwork.
- **VISUAL:** Detailed description of composition, characters, expressions, body language, movement, setting, lighting, and transitions.
- **INTERPRETIVE NOTE:** A cautious inference that is not literally written in the image.
- **TRANSCRIPTION NOTE:** A note about unusual or ambiguous printed wording.

### Recurring character labels used before or alongside formal identification

- **Kim Kyung-min:** A freshman boy with short brown hair and large round glasses; he is the chapter’s principal bullying victim.
- **Na Hwa-jin:** A tall, long-haired man in black, later identified as an officer of the Ministry of Education’s Office of Protection.
- **Purple-haired bully:** The most prominent student aggressor in the latter half of the chapter. The chapter does not unambiguously attach every spoken name to him, so this descriptive label is used where needed.
- **Vice-principal:** An older male administrator who confronts Na Hwa-jin near the chapter’s end.

# End of Chapter 1

## Chapter-level continuity summary

The chapter begins by presenting a legal and social crisis: after corporal punishment is prohibited, teachers report losing practical control over classrooms, students learn to exploit recordings and complaints, and one teacher is beaten to death. The government responds by revising protections for teachers and creating an unnamed mechanism that is revealed two years later as the **Office of Protection**.

At the school where the main story begins, sophomore **Park Dae-seok** dies by suicide after sustained bullying. The administration suppresses testimony and allows the death to be attributed to academic stress. Freshman **Kim Kyung-min**, who is being tortured and socially isolated by the same school culture, first hopes the police investigation will save him. When it instead demonstrates that even a death can be concealed, he attempts suicide from the school roof.

**Na Hwa-jin** interrupts the attempt. His first response is imperfect and dismissive—he suggests Kyung-min merely endure the remaining years—but Kyung-min forces him to confront the impossibility of endurance without help. Moments later, the purple-haired bully attacks Kyung-min directly in front of Na. Na responds with overwhelming physical force, ignores the students filming him, and exposes his Ministry of Education credential.

The chapter closes with Na announcing that the Office of Protection has been dispatched to investigate and “teach” a school whose students abuse one another and whose staff buried the circumstances of a child’s death. His authority, methods, and moral limits remain deliberately provocative as the next chapter is set up.
