# Chapter-level observations

## 1. Child protection requires accepting grief

Removing Min-seo is necessary, but it does not make him stop loving or missing his parents.

## 2. Dong-cheol’s most responsible act is leaving

For the first time, he prioritizes Min-seo’s safe environment over his own desire to be recognized as the father.

## 3. Temporary punishment can create a countdown for retaliation

Sang-wook’s aggressor treats the five-day suspension not as correction but as time to build anticipation.

## 4. The chapter connects two kinds of adult failure

One case involves parents unfit to raise a child. The new case begins with a school system whose disciplinary measure may have left a victim exposed immediately afterward.
