## Complete Detailed Panel-by-Panel Transcription

## Strip 001 — Na reveals how Tae-yang fell

Na says he intends to take back **Gayeon’s name**, linking the confrontation to the murdered person whose initials were appropriated by G.Y.

The others are stunned that Kim Tae-yang fell in one strike.

Tae-yang is described as the strongest active G.Y. fighter after the imprisoned chief.

Na hit the medulla or a similarly vulnerable neurological point with near-perfect accuracy.

A clean strike there can cause instant collapse regardless of the target’s physical strength.

A sharply dressed secretary steps forward.

The man is referred to as **Secretary Kim**.

He tells Yun-gyeol to stand back and draws a knife.

Na advises him to put it away.

The secretary says he will create an opening and tells his young master to escape.

Na observes that the man’s language makes clear this will not be an ordinary street brawl.

## Strip 002 — Secretary Kim enters “the battlefield”

Secretary Kim says he understands perfectly.

By drawing the weapon, he is stepping onto a battlefield.

He attacks with disciplined knife work rather than uncontrolled stabbing.

Na blocks, redirects the blade, and strikes at the secretary’s arms and balance.

The secretary transitions smoothly between attack and defense.

Na realizes that the man has trained for lethal combat.

Their movements fill the apartment corridor with repeated clashes.

Neither treats the exchange as a schoolyard fight.

## Strip 003 — The Professor and a Spetsnaz knife

Secretary Kim recognizes Na’s old military callsign:

> **PROFESSOR**

He asks whether Na also served in special forces.

Na identifies elements of Krav Maga and other practical combat systems in the secretary’s movement.

Secretary Kim says that, like Na and Hanrim, he did not come from an ordinary unit after enlistment.

He has served on the front lines of a civil war, though the country or conflict is obscured in the English scanlation.

The weapon itself resembles a Spetsnaz-style ballistic or combat knife.

The secretary combines knife thrusts with kicks, throws, and close-range strikes.

Na is knocked to the floor momentarily but continues fighting.

Yun-gyeol calls to Ma-ri:

> **NOW, MARI!**

## Strip 004 — Yun-gyeol and Ma-ri flee

Ma-ri and Yun-gyeol run toward the elevator while Secretary Kim occupies Na.

The secretary tells Na that if their skill were truly equal, Na would still be one level above him.

Even so, he believes he can hold the inspector while dividing his attention toward the fleeing pair.

Yun-gyeol admits that Na repeatedly makes him lose control.

The inspector survived the fire trap and then tracked the observation room.

Yun-gyeol says he underestimated him.

Ma-ri asks whether Secretary Kim will survive.

Yun-gyeol cannot guarantee victory, but says the secretary will not fall easily.

The organization will need far more preparation before confronting the agency again.

## Strip 005 — The groomed bodyguard fails to stop Na

Ma-ri explains that Secretary Kim was raised by Yun-gyeol’s family from childhood to become the perfect bodyguard.

He even entered a special-forces deployment to gain real combat experience.

Before the pair can escape completely, Na catches up.

Secretary Kim has been injured and temporarily put down.

Na seizes Yun-gyeol by the collar or throat.

He repeats his promise to reclaim Gayeon’s name.

Every person who dragged that name through criminal filth will be gathered and removed.

Na orders Yun-gyeol to contact all remaining former members and summon them into one place.

## Strip 006 — Yun-gyeol’s coercive childhood

Na’s hand around Yun-gyeol’s throat triggers a childhood memory.

A powerful adult stands over a younger Yun-gyeol and teaches obedience through fear.

The child is told:

- never to reveal his face carelessly;
- never to disgrace the family;
- and never to refuse the leash placed around him.

The adult compares a disobedient child to a dog with no reason to remain alive.

> **IF YOU DON’T COOPERATE, YOU’RE DEAD.**

In the present, Yun-gyeol lashes out.

> **DON’T ORDER ME AROUND.**

> **I WON’T LISTEN TO ANYONE…!**

> **I’M NOT HIM!**

Na says he is not joking.

He gives Yun-gyeol three seconds to cooperate.

## Strip 007 — Secretary Kim’s last attempt

Na begins counting.

> **ONE…**

> **TWO…**

At three, the elevator opens behind him.

Secretary Kim has recovered enough to reappear with a handgun.

Na notices the weapon at the final moment.

The secretary says that both fighters were too reckless.

He became so focused on stopping Na that his own movements widened and left openings.

He fires or prepares to fire at close range while advancing.

Na moves inside the line of the gun and counters.

A powerful strike lands against the side of Secretary Kim’s head or neck.

The bodyguard collapses face-first onto the floor.

The chapter ends with Na standing over him while Yun-gyeol’s last immediate protection is removed.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Secretary Kim is a trained combatant, not a ceremonial aide

His knife work, military history, and willingness to use a firearm show that the family created him as a weapon.

## Na’s old callsign carries a reputation

“Professor” identifies him as someone known for technical understanding and precision under combat conditions.

## Yun-gyeol’s need for control comes from earlier coercion

His refusal to obey Na is entangled with childhood treatment by an adult who threatened death for disobedience.

## The chapter links G.Y.’s internal abuse to its external cruelty

Yun-gyeol grew inside a hierarchy of leashes and disposable people, then built an organization that treats victims and subordinate branches the same way.

## Secretary Kim loses through overcommitment

His determination to stop Na causes the wide movement and exposed angle that Na exploits in the final counter.
