# Chapter-level observations

### 1. Lim attacks the infrastructure of abuse

She does not merely punish individual players. She ends compulsory dormitory living, rejects seniority during practice, and gives younger players permission to perform without deference.

### 2. The sisters’ public influence is part of their power

The fabricated photograph works initially because Soo-yeon’s celebrity image primes followers to defend her. The same network turns violently against Ja-yeon once contrary evidence appears.

### 3. The online crowd is not presented as justice

Netizens move directly from sympathy to doxxing and death threats. The chapter treats viral outrage as unstable and easily manipulated, even when it lands on a genuine perpetrator.

### 4. Lim’s final line reframes the accusation

Ja-yeon attempted to portray Lim as an abusive coach. Lim arrives with evidence that the “victim” is a school bully, forcing the case back onto the actual pattern of violence.
