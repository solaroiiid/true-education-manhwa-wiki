# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The “medicine” has impaired the skill it was supposed to improve

Hyunmin’s terror begins when he discovers that basic problems and familiar formulas are inaccessible. The mock exam demonstrates that the stimulant did not reveal hidden ability; dependence, overdose, sleep loss, and adverse effects have severely damaged his performance.

## The mother treats grades as a moral debt

She repeatedly describes the medication as something expensive that she “gave” him, making Hyunmin responsible for producing a return on investment. His apology is met with assault because she interprets his illness as ingratitude.

## Na transfers responsibility back to the adult

Instead of debating whether becoming a doctor is admirable, Na accepts her premise and applies it to her. If medical school is worth sacrificing a life for, she can sacrifice her own comfort and study herself. The locked study room turns Hyunmin’s daily confinement into a direct lesson.

## Hyunmin is removed before the mother is disciplined

Na first passes the injured student to Hanrim and orders immediate hospitalization. The confrontation with the mother occurs only after the child is physically separated from her, preventing her from using him as leverage during the argument.

## The chapter rejects productivity myths around non-prescribed stimulants

Na explicitly states that medication taken by people without ADHD can produce side effects and even reduce cognitive function. Within the story, the controlled mock exam serves as practical proof against the mother’s belief that better results justify any risk.
