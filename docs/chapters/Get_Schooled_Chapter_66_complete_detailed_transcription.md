# Reader page 1 — Yeri rides with the female helper

Han Yeri sits in the passenger seat while the female “helper” drives.

The woman says she ran away from home at fifteen and survived alone. Because of that history, she claims to understand girls who cannot return to their families.

She says she now helps only female runaways.

The story is carefully designed to counter every warning Yoo-jung gave her sister: this adult is a woman, presents herself as a former runaway, and offers a house without men.

# Reader page 3 — A crowded but welcoming residence

# Chapter-level observations

## 1. The operation mimics a family before revealing a market

Shared food, female companionship, and stories of common suffering are recruitment tools.

## 2. Victims can become recruiters

Some girls cooperate because the system has taught them that survival depends on passing harm downward to the next arrival.

## 3. “Women only” is a false safety signal

The female helper’s identity lowers suspicion, while male force remains hidden until resistance begins.

## 4. Yoon-jung’s existence is confirmed indirectly

The girls’ casual discussion of her punishment and work proves that the network knows where she has been sent, even though she is not yet present.
