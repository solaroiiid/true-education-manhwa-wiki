## Strips 005–006 — Dabin’s fake apology

Haeran says the exposure produced one apparent apology.

She displays a direct message from Dabin.

Dabin begins:

> **HYERAN, IT’S ME DABIN. I’M SORRY…**

She admits that she “picked on” Haeran and says she feels bad.

Haeran replies that Dabin bullied her for six years and participated in what happened to Yunju.

The apology immediately changes tone.

Dabin says Haeran is exaggerating the six-year period and argues that Yunju died two years after transferring, so the former group cannot be blamed.

Haeran sends the address of her studio and says that if Dabin truly wants to apologize, she should come in person, kneel before Yunju’s mother, and do it on camera.

Dabin refuses.

She asks:

> **DO YOU HAVE PROOF?**

She threatens to sue Haeran for defamation for revealing real names and workplaces.

Haeran tells viewers that this pattern is always the same: apology first, then minimization, demands for proof, and legal threats.

She asks classmates, seniors, juniors, and anyone who witnessed the abuse to submit statements.

Seoyoung dismisses that fear because her own father funded the Pilates studio.

Seoyoung remains convinced that the group can still control Haeran.

The livestream ends, but Haeran is shaken.

Dabin’s language and threats instantly revive the emotional atmosphere of school.

Haeran admits that she is scared to face the women again in any form.

Still:

> **I HAVE TO FIGHT.**

She wants to prove that a world where victims suffer forever while perpetrators forget is wrong.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Haeran’s case remains legally distinct from Yunju’s completed case

The public exposure cannot reopen every issue surrounding Yunju, but Haeran can still pursue offenses committed directly against her.

## Employment consequences arrive before adjudication

The chapter shows the power and danger of public exposure: workplaces respond to outrage even while formal proof remains contested.

## Dabin’s apology is conditional

She expresses regret only until Haeran asks for responsibility, public acknowledgment, and connection to Yunju’s death.

## Seoyoung understands evidentiary weakness

Her attack is designed to produce fear without marks, allowing her to accuse Haeran of inventing another story.

## Daniel anticipated the false-witness trap

His arrival indicates the agency has already begun monitoring the contact surrounding Haeran’s request for statements.
