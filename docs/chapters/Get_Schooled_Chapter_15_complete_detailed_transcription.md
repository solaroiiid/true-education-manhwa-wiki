## Images 001–026 — Na’s final lesson to Gwonhyeok

Na tells Gwonhyeok that his entire hierarchy was built on a false measure.

He assumed a strong person possesses greater human worth.

In practice, he used strength to crush students who could not fight back.

He never had to experience the fear he created until the construction-site ambush.

Na says Gwonhyeok was lucky.

> **NINE OUT OF TEN PEOPLE WOULD HAVE WATCHED THEM CUT YOUR HAND OFF.**

Hyeongju instead risked his body for the person who made his school life miserable.

That action required more character than any of Gwonhyeok’s victories.

> **PEOPLE ARE MEASURED BY CHARACTER, NOT STRENGTH.**

Na warns that he may return if Gwonhyeok fails to learn.

## Images 027–047 — Gwonhyeok apologizes without saying the word cleanly

He calls Hyeongju’s construction-site action impressive.

The praise is awkward because Gwonhyeok has spent years expressing emotion only through violence and rank.

Hyeongju accepts the sincerity beneath the clumsy language.

The school atmosphere begins changing.

## Images 062–079 — Na receives backup

At the Ministry, Na complains about the expanding backlog.

One inspector cannot personally investigate every case in the country.

Minister Choi Gang-seok says the agency has prepared additional support.

A red-haired woman enters with military posture.

She identifies herself as **Lim Hanrim**—the scanlation also renders her surname as Im in some dialogue—and states that she has been dispatched from a special-missions unit to the Teachers’ Rights Protection Agency.

Na recognizes her from military service.

She salutes and calls him:

> **CAPTAIN NA.**

Na is startled by her vivid red hair and changed appearance.

Hanrim says the color resembles how she looked during high school and may help her interact with students.

Their new assignment is:

> **SOYEON GIRLS’ HIGH SCHOOL**

## Images 080–096 — A male teacher is shaved and humiliated

At Soyeon Girls’ High, several students surround a male teacher.

> **MESSING WITH A TEACHER ISN’T FUN.**

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The automobile-company epilogue confirms lasting change

Gwonhyeok’s reform is shown through ordinary adulthood rather than another dramatic fight.

## Hanrim enters as a professional equal with a shared military past

Her familiarity with Na explains why she can challenge and joke with him immediately.

## Yeri’s power is procedural as well as social

She understands how student-protection language can be used to conceal teacher abuse.

## The new arc reverses the usual visible power arrangement

The adult appears physically stronger, yet employment rules and accusation risk leave him unable to defend himself.
