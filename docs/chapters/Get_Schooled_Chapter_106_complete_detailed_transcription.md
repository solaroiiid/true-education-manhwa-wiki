## Opening — Hwa-jin’s ordinary workload

The chapter opens with the standard fiction disclaimer. Na Hwa-jin is shown at another school handling a stream of Teachers’ Rights Protection Agency cases. Students protest while adults restrain or question them.

> **THIS CAN’T BE RIGHT!**
>
> **THE STUDENT ADMITTED THEIR MISTAKE!**
>
> **YOU GUYS ARE CROSSING THE LINE!**

Case files or captions identify incidents including assault on a teacher, sexual harassment of a female teacher, and cyberbullying. Hwa-jin looks increasingly drained as he flips through the workload.

> **I DON’T EVEN WANT TO THINK ABOUT ALL THE REPORTS I’LL HAVE TO WRITE…**

A small note explains that field agents may visit roughly two or three schools in a day. Hwa-jin grumbles that the load has exploded because his partner is absent.

> **MUST BE NICE FOR IM HAN LIM~**

He imagines Han-rim relaxing on her day off.

> **SHE’S PROBABLY ENJOYING HER DAY OFF.**

The chapter cuts immediately to the opposite reality: Han-rim, in a black suit, is in the cult compound surrounded by attackers.

## Han-rim confronts the pastor

Ju-hee watches Han-rim overpower adult cult members and thinks that, although Han-rim is a woman like her, she possesses overwhelming strength and authority.

> **THAT WOMAN IS WITHOUT A DOUBT…**
>
> **A WITCH WHO IS HERE TO END US ALL.**

Han-rim returns to the cult leader, who is visibly shaken.

> **SHALL WE CONTINUE OUR CONVERSATION FROM EARLIER, PASTOR?**
>
> **WITH THE REACTION YOU JUST DISPLAYED,**
>
> **I CAN ALMOST SMELL THE SHIT IN YOUR PANTS…**

The pastor tries to reject the ordinary title.

> **W-WHO ARE YOU CALLING A PASTOR?!**
>
> **I AM THE SON OF THE GOD WHO GOVERNS THE UNIVERSE!!**
>
> **AN AVATAR OF THE MESSIAH WHO DESCENDED ON THIS WORLD TO SAVE IT!**
>
> **IF YOU EVEN TOUCH A HAIR ON ME, YOU WILL BE CURS—**

Han-rim grabs him by the throat or collar and cuts off the sermon.

> **SAVE THAT BULLSHIT TALK FOR YOUR GOD.**
>
> **JUST ANSWER MY QUESTIONS!**
>
> **ARE THE WORDS OF THAT CHILD TRUE OR NOT?**

Her question concerns Ju-hee’s allegations of starvation, forced labor, educational neglect, abuse, and sexual misconduct.

---

## The children are activated as weapons

Instead of answering, the pastor begins reciting his fabricated scripture. Seo Yool, the little girl who previously handed Han-rim flowers, becomes blank-eyed and reaches for a sickle.

> **THE WITCH… IS HARMING THE SAVIOUR…**
>
> **THE TIME HAS COME…**

The pastor chants:

> **LORD’S NEW TESTAMENT, CHAPTER 3 VERSE 36…**
>
> **AS THE DEVIL WHO ESCAPED HELL STABS THE HEART OF THE SAVIOUR…**
>
> **THE BODIES OF THEIR BELIEVERS SHALL BATHE IN A HOLY LIGHT.**
>
> **LORD’S NEW TESTAMENT, CHAPTER 3 VERSE 37…**

Han-rim stares at him in disbelief.

> **WOW~ I CAN’T GET THROUGH TO HIM AT ALL!**

She calls him pathologically narcissistic.

> **YOU’RE INSANELY NARCISSISTIC…**
>
> **YOU REALLY CONVINCED YOURSELF THAT YOU’RE THE SON OF GOD?**
>
> **THAT’S IT. I’M TURNING YOU IN—**

Seo Yool swings the sickle. Han-rim intercepts the blade, receiving a shallow cut while preventing the child from completing the strike. The pastor continues framing violence as divine command.

> **ALL BELIEVERS ARE GRACED BY THE HOLY LIGHT. AS THE LORD COMMANDS THEM TO FIGHT.**

Han-rim’s expression turns from incredulity to fury.

> **YOU FUCKER…! HOW COULD YOU DO THIS TO THESE INNOCENT CHILDREN?!!**

## Ju-hyeok is ordered to attack his sister

A scream draws Han-rim’s attention. Ju-hee is restrained by other children while her younger brother Ju-hyeok approaches with a sickle. He has the same emptied, indoctrinated stare.

> **G-GUYS… W-WHAT ARE YOU DOING?!**
>
> **JU HYEOK!!**
>
> **JU HYEOK… DON’T DO THIS…**
>
> **I’M YOUR SISTER!!**

The pastor recites another verse:

> **LORD’S NEW TESTAMENT, CHAPTER 3 VERSE 38…**
>
> **ALL BELIEVERS ARE THE SOLDIERS OF GOD!**
>
> **LET THEM SINK THEIR SCYTHES INTO THE DEMONS!**
>
> **SPILL THE DEMON’S BLOOD!**

Ju-hyeok raises the blade. Ju-hee’s face is shown in extreme terror as the religious command overrides family recognition.

Han-rim lunges between them. She knocks the child off balance and grabs him around the head and upper body, stopping the attack without striking him as she would an adult. The sickle falls onto the grass beside scattered flowers.

> **NO!!**

Ju-hee collapses nearby, realizing Han-rim has saved both her and her brother.

---

## The cult closes around them

More adult believers gather in a semicircle. Their faces are blank, angry, or ecstatic. The pastor scratches or traces a pentagram-like symbol into his clothing while continuing his invented scripture.

> **LORD’S NEW TESTAMENT, CHAPTER 3 VERSE 39…**
>
> **LORD, SAINT, BELIEVER,**
>
> **ALL THREE SHALL UNITE AGAINST THE EVIL…**
>
> **AND DEFEAT IT ONCE AND FOR ALL.**

Han-rim rises in front of Ju-hee and Ju-hyeok. Her eyes sharpen, and she introduces herself with deliberate confidence:

> **I’M HAN LIM~**

The declaration functions as both reassurance to the children and a warning to the surrounding cultists.

## Hwa-jin notices the silence

The scene cuts back to the agency office. Hwa-jin cheerfully raises a hand toward Han-rim’s empty chair.

> **HOW WAS YOUR DAY OFF?**
>
> **ARE YOU READY TO WORK YOUR ASS OFF?**
>
> **YOU HAVE NO IDEA HOW MUCH I SUFFERED WITHOUT YO—**

He finally notices she is not there.

> **WHAT’S THIS?**
>
> **SHE’S LATE?**

Hwa-jin becomes annoyed rather than immediately afraid.

> **I GAVE HER THE DAY OFF SO THAT SHE COULD REFRESH HERSELF!**
>
> **SHE SHOULD BE GRATEFUL AND COME EARLY… HOW DARE SHE BE LATE…**

He calls her while smoking. The automated message answers:

> **THE PERSON YOU CALLED CANNOT BE REACHED, PLEASE LEAVE A VOICE MESSAGE AFTER THE BEEP.**

Hwa-jin’s eyes shift sharply.

> **?!**
>
> **WHAT’S THIS?**

He remembers a crucial fact: Han-rim’s formal profession is special-forces soldier, and soldiers do not simply switch off their phones when emergencies may arise.

## Cliffhanger — Han-rim captured

The final image reveals Han-rim and Ju-hee kneeling in a dark room. Both are dirty, bruised, and bloodied. Their hands appear restrained behind them; a chain runs from the wall or floor behind their bodies. Han-rim’s suit and white shirt are torn and stained, but her eyes remain fiercely alert.

The pastor stands before them with adult followers behind him, smiling in triumph.

Hwa-jin’s thought or spoken line overlays the scene:

> **A SOLDIER TURNING OFF HER PHONE?**

The chapter ends on Han-rim lifting her eyes from beneath blood-matted red hair, confirming that Hwa-jin’s suspicion is correct: the “day off” has become a hostage situation.

---

## End of Chapter 106

The chapter turns the cult’s indoctrination into direct physical danger. Children are not merely neglected; they are conditioned to interpret violence against family members as holy duty. Han-rim protects them but is eventually overwhelmed or captured off-panel, while Hwa-jin begins to recognize that her silence is an emergency signal.
