# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Soo-hyun joined for friendship, not delinquency

Her repeated school transfers and social anxiety made online chat rooms feel safer. The same openness that helped her adapt also exposed her to a group that tested loyalty through humiliation and violence.

## The attackers manufacture “mutual assault”

They summon a crowd, chant “fight,” tell the victim to strike back, and record selectively. The public claim that both sides participated is therefore built into the setup before the first punch.

## So-rin’s betrayal is the emotional center

Soo-hyun follows because she sees So-rin as her closest friend and believes she will mediate. So-rin instead delivers her into the ambush and controls the filming.

## The crowd expands the harm

Soo-hyun distinguishes anger at the direct attackers from horror at strangers who laugh, film, repost, and later judge her. The violence continues after the physical event through comments and coordinated pressure.

## Juvenile status becomes a shield

Hee-soo openly says serious injury does not matter because juvenile offenders will receive little punishment. Na’s arrival challenges that confidence through an educational intervention outside the girls’ expected consequences.
