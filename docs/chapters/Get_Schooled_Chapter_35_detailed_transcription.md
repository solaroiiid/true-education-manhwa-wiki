## Strip 003 — Cho Kyu-cheol begins his story

Na sits opposite Cho Kyu-cheol. Cho answers Na’s unspoken demand to explain what happened to Choi Ga-yoon.

> “WAS IT IN MAY…?”

> “IT WAS A VERY FUCKING HOT DAY IN MAY.”

> “THE STUPID SCHOOL WOULDN’T TURN ON THE AC BEFORE JUNE.”

> “I WAS ANNOYED, SO I WAS GONNA SKIP SCHOOL.”

# Chapter-level observations

### 1. Cho’s confession is structured as blame displacement

He does not deny killing Ga-yoon’s teacher; he converts heat, air-conditioning policy, and the victim’s intervention into excuses. His repeated “if only” statements demonstrate a complete refusal of moral agency.

### 2. Na experiences the victim’s family’s legal helplessness

The scene is not about whether Cho deserves anger. It is about Na discovering that anger cannot lawfully be converted into a punch once the offender is already in custody.

### 3. The new arc begins through propaganda-like sports coverage

The sisters are introduced as national treasures before the private abuse is revealed. Their public teamwork, beauty, and follower count give them protection within the school.

### 4. The cliffhanger establishes Lim Han-rim as the answer

Eun-ha is told nobody will help her. The immediate cut to Lim outside the school turns that threat into dramatic irony.
