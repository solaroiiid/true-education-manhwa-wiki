## Reader Images 001–002 — Daniel confronts Suchan about becoming an abuser

Daniel stands in the washroom after witnessing Suchan lead the humiliation of another student.

He says:

> **I HEARD THAT YOU USED TO BE BULLIED A LOT TOO.**

Suchan’s smile becomes guarded.

Daniel asks:

> **HAVE YOU BECOME JUST LIKE THEM IN THE END…?**

Suchan answers with false innocence:

> **WHAT DO YOU MEAN, MASTER?**

> **THIS IS JUST A PRANK WE SOMETIMES PULL.**

> **JUST A CHILDISH PRANK…**

Daniel turns the language back on him:

> **YEAH, THE ONES WHO BULLIED YOU BEFORE PROBABLY SAID THE SAME.**

> **IT WAS JUST A PRANK!**

> **HOW DID YOU FEEL WHEN YOU HEARD THAT…?**

Suchan cannot answer before an adult voice interrupts.

## Reader Images 005–007 — Daniel reports the bullying directly

Inside the principal’s office, Daniel asks:

## Reader Images 008–010 — The principal calls abuse ordinary adolescence

The principal responds calmly:

> **THEY ARE ADOLESCENTS.**

> **THEY’RE ALL FULL OF ENERGY, SO THIS CAN HAPPEN.**

He argues that adults should not interfere:

> **INTERFERENCE BY ADULTS IN CHILDREN’S MATTERS ONLY MAKES THE SITUATION WORSE, DOESN’T IT?**

Daniel objects:

> **BUT TO LEAVE IT BE WITH THE BULLYING BEING THIS SEVERE…**

The principal’s pleasant expression disappears.

> **MR. HYUN, LISTEN WELL TO ME.**

> **HERE, I AM BOTH THE LAW**

> **AND THE SCRIPTURE.**

> **YOU MUST FOLLOW MY WORDS UNCONDITIONALLY.**

> **DO YOU UNDERSTAND?**

He offers Daniel only submission or departure:

> **IF YOU DON’T LIKE IT, YOU’RE FREE TO LEAVE RIGHT NOW.**

Then, with a courteous smile:

> **HAVE A COMFORTABLE NIGHT, MASTER HYUN.**

The conversation confirms that the principal’s philosophy of noninterference is institutional permission for violence.

> **IF THINGS CONTINUE THIS WAY, MY ABILITY TO ACT FREELY WILL BE SEVERELY LIMITED.**

> **THE SITUATION IS SERIOUS, AND WE CAN’T JUST WATCH ANY LONGER. INTERVENE.**

Daniel hesitates:

> **WHAT? BUT THE PRINCIPAL…**

Na answers:

> **I’LL HANDLE THE PRINCIPAL.**

> **DURING THAT TIME, FEEL FREE TO DO WHAT YOU WANT!**

Daniel says:

> **I’M NOT SURE HOW YOU’LL DO IT, BUT I’LL TRUST YOU, SENIOR.**

Na asks whether Daniel is prepared for the target:

> **YOU’RE TARGETING SOMEONE WHO HAS EXPERIENCED BOTH BEING A VICTIM AND AN AGGRESSOR.**

> **HE DOESN’T SEEM LIKE SOMEONE WHO CAN BE PERSUADED JUST WITH WORDS, DOES HE?**

Daniel believes Na misunderstood his interview:

> **IT SEEMS YOU’VE MISUNDERSTOOD WHAT I SAID DURING THE INTERVIEW.**

> **I SAID I WOULDN’T USE VIOLENCE IN TEACHING…**

> **BUT THAT DOESN’T MEAN I’M POWERLESS.**

His hand closes with visible strength. The distinction is deliberate: he refuses punitive violence as a teaching method, not physical intervention or control.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The principal’s theology is a tool of absolute authority

His claim to be both “law” and “scripture” reveals why the school’s spiritual language cannot restrain abuse. Every moral principle is subordinate to the principal’s interpretation, and disobedience can be redefined as impurity or failure.

## Suchan uses the same minimization once used against him

Calling the assault a “childish prank” protects him from recognizing that he has become part of the structure that tortured him. Daniel’s question—how did the old excuse feel when directed at you?—forces continuity between victim and perpetrator.

## Institutional endorsement gives Suchan confidence

He is not afraid of expulsion because he knows the principal sees student-on-student violence as useful. His rank depends on enforcing the school’s culture, not violating it.

## The birthday scene shows recruitment through participation

Suchan does not become an aggressor in one unexplained leap. Older students invite him to strike someone as a marker of maturity and belonging. Refusal would return him to the bottom of the hierarchy; participation moves him upward.

## Daniel’s nonviolence allows coercive tactics

He rejects beating as instruction, but he is willing to use darkness, surprise, restraint, and psychological pressure. “Not gentle” signals that his alternative method may still be frightening and forceful.

## Na and Daniel divide the operation

Na promises to neutralize the principal’s authority while Daniel handles the students. This prevents Daniel’s undercover dismissal from ending the intervention and sets up parallel confrontations with the institution’s adult and student leadership.
