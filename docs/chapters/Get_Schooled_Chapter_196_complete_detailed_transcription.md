## Strip 001 — The court awards ₩200 million

The court finds that Lee Janghyup breached a lawyer’s fundamental professional duties.

The judge emphasizes that lawyers occupy a position requiring unusually strict responsibility because clients entrust them with rights they cannot protect alone.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The award separates difficulty from duty

The original case may have been hard to win. That does not erase the damage caused by failing to preserve the client’s opportunity to be heard.

## The mother interprets judgment as recognition

The money cannot raise Yunju. Its symbolic value is the country finally acknowledging that the second institutional injury mattered.

## Reform and ambition coexist

Kim Taeseon’s political benefit does not automatically make the disciplinary reform false. The chapter deliberately refuses a clean separation between principle and self-interest.

## Choi uses connections rather than issuing unlawful orders on-panel

He creates visibility and incentive around the case while claiming each institution made its own formal decision.

## The lawyer’s coming conflict is about authorship

He needs to know whether the victory belonged to evidence and advocacy or to powerful people quietly arranging the environment.
