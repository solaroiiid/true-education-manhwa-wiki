# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The review committee is defeated by narrative manipulation

The girls transform a planned ambush into “mutual assault” by claiming Soo-hyun fought back and caused the dispute. The relatively mild penalties become proof, in their minds, that they can continue.

## Suspension functions as publicity rather than correction

They travel, post fashionable images, and assault more students for content. Removal from school gives them more time to cultivate attention.

## The fabricated agency assault exploits public skepticism

Bruises are repackaged as evidence that adult men attacked powerless girls. The story spreads because it is emotionally legible online even without verification.

## So-rin’s alleged evidence is central

Hee-soo’s grievance depends on So-rin’s claim that Soo-hyun insulted her behind her back. When Soo-hyun demands proof, So-rin cannot produce it and becomes visibly frightened.

## Na chooses public transparency as punishment

Because the girls use editing and selective clips, he announces unedited live guidance. The agency will confront them in the same arena where they built their influence, but without surrendering control of the narrative.
