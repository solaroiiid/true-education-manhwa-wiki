## Images 001–003 — Hanrim identifies herself

Hanrim holds the bloodied Lee Cheolyong by his shirt.

She formally introduces herself:

> **FIELD SUPERVISOR FROM THE TEACHERS’ RIGHTS PROTECTION AGENCY,**

> **LIM HANRIM.**

Cheolyong’s eyes widen:

> **TRPA…?**

Hanrim tells the teacher:

> **MISS, COME WITH US TOO…**

> **I WANNA GET A CLEAR STATEMENT OF THE ASSAULT.**

She orders the two accompanying students to follow as witnesses.

Yu Seoyeon hesitates and asks:

> **COULD YOU PLEASE TURN A BLIND EYE TO THE MATTER THIS TIME?**

Hanrim looks back, surprised.

## Images 004–006 — Seoyeon asks her to let it go

Seoyeon says:

> **WHAT CAN YOU DO IF THEY WON’T ACCEPT A HELPING HAND EVEN WHEN OFFERED?**

> **THERE’S PEOPLE LIKE THAT WHO JUST WON’T ACCEPT SOMEONE’S GOODWILL—**

Hanrim answers that the students are simply acting according to their own convenience.

Seoyeon says:

> **JUST FORGET ABOUT IT, UNNIE—**

Then pleads directly:

> **IT’S A REQUEST!!**

> **JUST ONCE… PLEASE JUST IGNORE IT THIS ONCE.**

Hanrim refuses.

She states:

> **THIS IS AN EXTREMELY SERIOUS INFRINGEMENT OF TEACHERS’ RIGHTS.**

> **AS A SUPERVISOR FROM THE TEACHERS’ RIGHTS PROTECTION AGENCY, I JUST CAN’T…**

Seoyeon replies that Hanrim seems to be doing it for the children’s sake and says she can side with them if she wants.

Hanrim’s internal response is severe:

> **SUCH BLATANT VIOLATORS OF TEACHERS’ RIGHTS OUGHT TO BE GIVEN A PROPER LESSON…**

> **DO SUBSTITUTE TEACHERS LIKE ME EVEN HAVE TEACHERS’ RIGHTS?**

The question becomes the case’s central issue.

During the day, Hanrim and Seoyeon go shopping.

Seoyeon behaves as though the previous night should be forgotten. She opens the door of a clean clothing or accessory shop and excitedly suggests outfits.

Hanrim tries on a blue wig or accessory as Seoyeon encourages her to choose something suitable for a sticker-photo booth.

The chapter shifts to an undercover plan.

## Images 010–012 — Lee Cheolyong returns to school

Cheolyong walks toward school with his face heavily bandaged.

His friends laugh:

> **AYY, YOU’RE LUCKY~**

> **AFTER GETTING CAUGHT BY THE TRPA, YOU SHOULD BE GRATEFUL THAT IT ONLY RESULTED IN THIS.**

> **BULLCRAP~**

> **HELLO, MY NAME IS YOO SEOYEON.**

Cheolyong passes without recognizing her.

He laughs that substitutes cannot be full teachers and are easy to control.

## Images 013–015 — The undercover identity nearly slips

A permanent teacher named **Lee Seoni** is assigned to guide the new substitute.

She tells Hanrim that she will be responsible for the vacant second-year class and asks which school Hanrim previously worked at.

Hanrim answers that this is her first assignment.

Lee Seoni looks at her closely:

> **OH? YOUR VOICE… I’VE HEARD IT BEFORE…**

> **HAVE WE MET BEFORE?**

Hanrim briefly panics that she has been recognized, then denies it.

Her internal plan is simple: borrow Yu Seoyeon’s identity long enough to investigate how the school treats temporary teachers.

Lee Seoni warns her that the students may make fun of her appearance and that a permanent position might be better.

The disguised Hanrim thinks:

## Images 019–022 — The second rule is broken with a serve

Hanrim recalls the second survival rule:

> **SECOND RULE:**

> **NO MATTER HOW THE KIDS REACT, ABSOLUTELY DO NOT CAUSE ANY TROUBLE.**

> **EVEN IF THAT HAPPENS, THERE’S NOTHING WE CAN DO.**

> **AFTER ALL, WE’RE JUST SUBSTITUTES WHO CAN BE FIRED ANYTIME.**

Blood erupts around his bandages.

He falls into equipment while his friends shout his name.

Cheolyong thinks:

> **HUH…? WHAT’S THIS FAMILIAR SHOCK!**

Hanrim smiles.

She has blatantly broken both substitute-survival rules.

> **ALRIGHT~ LET’S SEE WHAT HAPPENS NOW, SHALL WE?**

> **THAT’S WHAT I CALL A SERVE.**

The chapter ends on her satisfied expression.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Seoyeon’s request is rooted in employment fear

She does not deny the assault. She believes reporting it will harm the temporary teacher more than the students.

## Hanrim’s disguise tests the system directly

Rather than investigating only past testimony, she enters as the kind of worker the school considers disposable.

## Revealing substitute status is bait

Hanrim gives the students information they normally use to identify safe targets.

## The serve creates undeniable evidence

The strike is excessive and intentional, but it forces administrators to show whose violence they consider actionable: a substitute teacher’s single blow or the students’ continuing harassment.
