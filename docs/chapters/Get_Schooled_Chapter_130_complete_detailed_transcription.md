## Complete Detailed Panel-by-Panel Transcription

### Scope

This transcription follows all **23 ordered reader images** in the uploaded Chapter 130 archive. It preserves the readable English scanlation text, including imperfect grammar and word choices. Prominent Korean sound effects are described according to the visible action they accompany.

### Content note

The chapter discusses suicide, self-harm, stimulant-related cognitive impairment, parental coercion, and drug overdose.

## Reader Images 001–002 — Na explains why Hyunmin could eventually attack her

The chapter resumes with Hyunmin’s mother still holding a knife after being restrained by Na Hwa-jin.

She stares at the hypothetical image of Hyunmin attacking her and protests:

> **WH-WHAT ARE YOU TALKING ABOUT? WHY WOULD MY SON HARM ME…?**

Na tells her to examine her own reaction:

> **LOOK AT YOURSELF NOW.**

> **DIDN’T YOU PICK UP A KNIFE JUST BECAUSE YOU WERE FORCED TO STUDY FOR BARELY A WEEK?**

He contrasts her short confinement with what she imposed on her son:

> **HYUNMIN HAS BEEN ENDURING THIS KIND OF PRESSURE FOR THREE WHOLE YEARS.**

> **MOREOVER, IF HE DOESN’T GET INTO MEDICAL SCHOOL, HE’LL HAVE TO REPEAT THE YEAR, MAYBE EVEN TWO OR THREE TIMES.**

Na’s expression is grave rather than teasing.

> **IF THIS CONTINUES, HYUNMIN MIGHT END UP HARMING YOU OR TAKING HIS OWN L*FE.**

The mother’s hand loosens around the knife.

> **WHY HAVE THEY TIED UP HYUNMIN?!**

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Hyunmin’s restraints are protective, not disciplinary

His mother interprets the restraints as interference with study. The flashback shows that he was actively trying to hurt himself because cognitive side effects made him believe his future had disappeared.

## The mother’s ambition is rooted in resentment toward her own life

She believes her parents failed by not choosing a path for her. Na’s response is that she has converted regret into intergenerational control. Her child cannot be assigned the task of repairing her biography.

## Na distinguishes external circumstances from another person’s dream

Na entered the military academy because poverty narrowed his options, but he researched the institution and chose a path that would make him independent. Hyunmin, by contrast, is forced toward medical school because his mother wants the status attached to it.

## Rest becomes evidence of abuse

Hyunmin falls asleep the moment permission is granted. The scene makes clear that the mother did not merely encourage long hours; she trained him to interpret basic sleep as failure.

## The arc ends by widening the frame

Even after Hyunmin is protected, another overdose reaches the hospital. Statistics about prescriptions and illegal sales show that the competitive education system continues producing demand for dangerous shortcuts.

## Daniel’s arrival bridges into the next arc

Daniel’s return at the airport follows his introduction in Chapter 125 and signals that the Teachers’ Rights Protection Agency’s mixed-race supervisor will rejoin the active storyline.
