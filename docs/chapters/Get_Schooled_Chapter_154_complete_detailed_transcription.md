# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The retreat is a withdrawal period, not a miraculous cure

Na explicitly says the temple stay itself is “meaningless.” Its purpose is to separate So-rin from constant metrics for one week so that the platform’s attention cycle can move on without her.

## The monk combines spiritual language with military discipline

Jin-nyeom teaches awareness, food discipline, meditation, and appreciation of nature, but enforces rules with the command voice and physical intensity of a former special-forces chaplain. The method is deliberately harsher than an ordinary retreat.

## The repaired phone is part of the test

Na does not permanently confiscate technology. He returns it and allows So-rin to discover that the audience was attached to the controversy, not to her as a person.

## Social-media attention is shown as disposable

Within a week, several unrelated scandals have replaced So-rin in the trends. Praise and hatred both vanish because users seek the next emotionally stimulating event.

## Legal consequences continue after public guidance

The agency’s livestream correction, account ban, and temple intervention do not replace juvenile proceedings. Hee-soo and So-rin still face protective custody based on the original assault and subsequent conduct.

## The final irony

The girls once believed they could control every story by selecting angles, comments, and clips. In juvenile court, the official record determines the outcome, and they experience what it means to have no control over the frame.
