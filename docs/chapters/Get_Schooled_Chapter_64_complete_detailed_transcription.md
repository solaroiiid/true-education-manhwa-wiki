# Chapter-level observations

## 1. The word “helper” is the disguise

Predators borrow the language of empathy because runaway children already expect rejection from legitimate institutions.

## 2. Yeri’s experience is operational knowledge

Her history makes her an unusually effective decoy, but it also exposes her again to the type of adult who previously harmed her.

## 3. Refusal to stop reveals the real relationship

The BMW driver’s moral speech collapses the moment Yeri asks to leave. A helper allows choice; an exploiter removes it.

## 4. The unidentified dead girl prevents a simple rescue narrative

Yoon-jung may still be alive, but the system has already killed another child.
