## Strips 001–003 — Na visits the imprisoned G.Y. boss

Na enters a prison visitation room.

The long-haired founder of G.Y., **Jo Gyuchul**, recognizes him immediately.

> **NA… HWAJIN…?!**

Na sits across the glass.

> **I AM HERE WITH TWO THINGS.**

> **THESE ARE A WARNING AND A REQUEST.**

Gyuchul asks why the man he wants dead has voluntarily come to prison.

Na says he should be the angry one after G.Y. attacked former agency victims, placed bounties on inspectors, and endangered children.

Gyuchul slams the glass and curses him.

Na tells him to sit.

If the boss truly wants him dead, Na says, he should stop sending scattered amateurs and gather every loyal faction in one place.

> **I’LL CLEAN UP ALL YOUR DAMN RIFFRAFF WHILE I’M AT IT.**

Gyuchul taunts Na with an old killing and tries to provoke a personal reaction.

Na says he is sorry for the lives G.Y. destroyed, but refuses to give the boss the emotional scene he wants.

## Strips 004–006 — Na turns the prison network into a trap

Gyuchul asks what Na really wants.

Na says he finally found the person capable of contacting the remaining loyalists.

That person is Gyuchul himself.

The imprisoned boss still controls members across the country through visitors, illegal phones, attorneys, and branch intermediaries.

Na tells him to summon them.

Gyuchul says he will call every loyal member and order them to bring Na’s head.

Na answers that the instruction is exactly what he expects.

He warns:

> **THE DAY YOU STEP OUT OF PRISON WILL BE THE DAY YOU DIE.**

The threat is less a promise of murder than notice that the boss’s organization, protection, and power will be gone by then.

Gyuchul uses a hidden or smuggled phone.

He orders supporters nationwide to act by the next day.

A guard discovers activity around the cell, but the message has already gone out.

## Strips 007–009 — Yoon-kyul’s faction benefits from Na’s response

The chapter moves to G.Y.’s strategic faction.

Mari and Ji Yoon-kyul discuss the mobilization.

They call the operation a success.

Their plan is to use the TRPA to eliminate the imprisoned boss’s most loyal and violent branches.

Mari risked herself during the bounty campaign, allowed police to capture disposable participants, and used the resulting chaos to map reactions across the organization.

Yoon-kyul says:

> **THIS WAS A TOTAL SUCCESS.**

The pro-boss faction will blindly obey Gyuchul’s order.

Once they gather and attack Na, the government will arrest or destroy them.

This removes Yoon-kyul’s internal rivals without forcing his own faction into a civil war.

He says the “ape” in prison will reveal every loyal face himself.

Seo Changho had been especially troublesome because he led the action squad and openly supported the boss.

Now Changho is imprisoned and the remaining loyalists are being exposed.

Yoon-kyul smiles:

> **ONCE I ELIMINATE ALL OF THAT APE’S FACTION USING THE TRPA…**

> **G.Y. WILL BE ALL MINE.**

Na has moved the monster, but Yoon-kyul believes he controls where it attacks.

## Strip 010 — A new case begins with an absent lawyer

The narrative shifts away from G.Y.

A court announces:

> **THIRD APPEAL HEARING BEGINS.**

A middle-aged woman sits alone at the plaintiff’s table, checking her phone repeatedly.

The plaintiff’s attorney is absent.

Court staff warn that this is already another failed appearance.

The woman calls desperately:

> **PLEASE WAIT… I’LL TRY CALLING HIM.**

The phone rings beside a sleeping person who does not answer.

The woman begins crying.

The captions introduce a long-running civil case involving school violence and a dead child.

The chapter ends with the plaintiff facing the possibility that years of litigation may collapse because the lawyer entrusted with the case has again failed to appear.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Na weaponizes Gyuchul’s authority

The boss believes he is renewing the assassination order. In practice, he identifies every faction still loyal enough to obey.

## Yoon-kyul benefits from both sides

The pro-boss members expose themselves, while the TRPA takes the legal and physical risk of defeating them.

## Gyuchul’s prison status is not symbolic

He retains phones, visitors, attorneys, and national influence. Imprisonment has not ended command.

## Na and Yoon-kyul each think they are using the other

Na wants the loyal factions gathered. Yoon-kyul wants those same factions erased so he can inherit G.Y.

## The final strip begins a legal-abuse arc

The new plaintiff is not fighting a student or gang directly. She is fighting delay, procedural abandonment, and a lawyer whose absences may end the case.
