## Strip 006 — “I only call you Dad because there is no other word”

Yunseong lists everything the man never did.

He never:

- gave regular allowance;
- cooked meals;
- helped with school;
- corrected him consistently;
- protected him;
- or behaved like a parent.

> **I ONLY CALL YOU DAD BECAUSE I HAVE NO OTHER WORD.**

The accusation strips away the father’s belief that biological status automatically creates authority.

The child’s physical attacks are weaker than the former boxer’s old punches, but the father cannot control him through strength while injured.

For the first time, he realizes that fists are not a complete parenting method.

Yunseong attempts to leave.

The exact mechanics are stylized, but the household becomes physically dangerous.

Na intervenes and catches the father by the collar.

## Strip 008 — What it means to be a parent

The father says his hands are broken.

He cannot beat Yunseong into obedience.

Na answers that this limitation exists because the man raised the child to understand only violence.

> **THEN FIND ANOTHER WAY.**

No excuse will erase the responsibility.

The father has two choices:

- set the child straight no matter how difficult the process becomes;
- or bear the consequences of the child’s continued offending.

> **THAT’S WHAT IT MEANS TO BE A PARENT.**

The chapter ends with the parent confronting a role he has never genuinely performed.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Police contact is not family repair

Collecting Yunseong once does not create trust or authority.

## The father immediately returns to avoidance

Alcohol and socializing remain easier than learning what the child needs.

## Yunseong’s accusation is emotionally accurate

His own crimes remain his responsibility, but the title “father” has not been supported by years of parental action.

## Violence has lost its effectiveness

The broken hands force the former boxer to consider methods he never developed.

## Na defines responsibility as persistence

Parenting is not one punishment or one affectionate gesture. It is the obligation to remain involved through repeated failure.
