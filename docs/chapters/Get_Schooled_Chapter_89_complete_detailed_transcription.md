Before the formal discussion, the children present their work.

The classroom atmosphere is warm and ordinary.

Woo-jin’s mother is still absent as the meeting approaches its scheduled end.

# A special class on apology and forgiveness

At the last moment, Woo-jin’s mother arrives.

She asks for a temporary special lesson.

> “THE SUBJECT IS APOLOGY AND FORGIVENESS.”

She identifies herself as Lee Woo-jin’s mother.

In front of the teacher, children, and parents, she admits that she made false complaints and used Woo-jin.

She says Mrs. Choi lost weight and suffered because of her actions.

She bows and sincerely asks everyone for forgiveness.

For a moment, the public confession appears to fulfill the agency’s condition.

# The parents attack

The room erupts.

Parents call her inhuman for using her own child.

They demand that Woo-jin transfer, that his mother leave the neighborhood, and that she be sent to prison.

One says her child cannot attend the same school as the son of such a woman.

The apology has not restored her status. It has formally confirmed every accusation.

# Her performance collapses

Woo-jin’s mother realizes that enduring the humiliation for a few minutes will not automatically restore her social position.

She decides that if she loses here, she will suffer for the rest of her life.

Rather than accept the consequences, she attacks the other mothers.

She says many of them also called Choi at night, complained, and gossiped in private group messages.

She threatens to reveal their chats.

The parents turn on one another and begin fighting.

The woman’s apology is exposed as another attempt to control the outcome rather than genuine acceptance of wrongdoing.

# Mrs. Choi asks for restraint

The boy is embarrassed, angry, and exhausted by the adult spectacle.

# Woo-jin asks the agency for help

Woo-jin tells the Teachers’ Rights Protection Agency that his mother has behaved this way for a long time.

He says people like her cannot be fixed through ordinary arguments.

He begs:

> “PLEASE CATCH MY MOM.”

> “PLEASE MAKE MY MOM HUMAN.”

The chapter ends with the child asking for protection from the parent who repeatedly claimed to act on his behalf.

# Chapter-level observations

## 1. Public apology is not automatic reconciliation

The people harmed are not required to forgive simply because the perpetrator completes a prescribed performance.

## 2. The apology fails when status does not return

The mother can tolerate bowing only if it ends the consequences. When it does not, she resumes attacking others.

## 3. Other parents are not entirely innocent

Her accusations about their late calls and gossip may be self-serving, but the earlier chapters show that several did participate.

## 4. Woo-jin becomes the clearest victim

The adults debate punishment and reputation while he asks for a mother capable of recognizing him as a person.
