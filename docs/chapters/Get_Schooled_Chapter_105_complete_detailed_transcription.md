## Images 2–5 — The “school” is revealed as a rehearsed performance

### Disclaimer

> **THIS IS A WORK OF FICTION.**  
> **ANY SIMILARITY TO ACTUAL PERSONS,**  
> **LIVING OR DEAD, OR ACTUAL EVENTS,**  
> **IS PURELY COINCIDENTAL.**

The chapter resumes with Han-rim crouched beside the frightened little girl while the pastor stands behind them. The girl has accidentally called Han-rim **“Mister Police.”**

The pastor hesitates, then smiles smoothly.

> **HAHA… WELL, ABOUT THAT…**

> **TRUTH IS, THE POLICE PAID US A VISIT ONCE.**

> **LIKE EARLIER, SOME PEOPLE MISUNDERSTOOD OUR INTENTIONS AND HAD REPORTED US.**

Ju-hee’s internal thought appears in a jagged balloon:

> **A SLIP UP…!**

The pastor claims the child’s phrase came from practice.

> **THAT JUST NOW WAS A RESULT OF REPEATED PRACTICE!**

> **HENCE WHY SHE MISTOOK YOU FOR A POLICE OFFICER.**

> **DON’T KIDS USUALLY STICK THE TITLE MISTER WHEN THEY REFER TO POLICE OFFICERS?**

Han-rim stares at him without answering. Her eyes narrow, but she still lacks proof.

A flashback exposes the truth behind his explanation. The little girl is slapped violently across the face by an adult handler.

> **YOU FUCKING BITCH!**

The handler grabs her hair and forces her face upward.

> **WHAT DID I TELL YOU? THE POLICE HAVE TO GO BACK IN A GOOD MOOD SO THAT WE CAN AVOID BACKLASH!**

> **BUT IF YOU MAKE SUCH AN UNNATURAL EXPRESSION, WILL THEY FEEL GOOD? HUH?!**

> **NOW, DO IT AGAIN! BE CUTER!**

The child repeats the line with a painfully broad smile while holding flowers:

> **COME BACK SOON, MISTER POLICE OFFICER~**

Another adult turns toward the other children.

> **YOU TOO, YOU LITTLE SHITE!**

> **QUIT SMILING LIKE A BUNCH OF RETARDS AND ASK SOME QUESTIONS!**

> **LIKE A REAL CLASS!**

Narration explains the elaborate deception:

> **DUE TO US BEING A PSEUDO RELIGIOUS CULT, THE POLICE WOULD VISIT US.**

> **AN ACT CREATED IN ORDER TO FOOL THE POLICE…**

> **AFTER COUNTLESS HOURS OF STRICT AND REPETITIVE TRAINING, WE MANAGED TO IMITATE A REAL CLASS ATMOSPHERE.**

> **AND IT WAS A SUCCESS.**

A uniformed officer accepts flowers from a smiling child.

**Child:**

> **COME BACK SOON, MISTER POLICE OFFICER~**

**Officer:**

> **OH MY~ THANK YOU~**

The staged friendliness has repeatedly convinced outsiders that the compound is safe.

## Images 6–8 — Ju-hee silently begs Han-rim to notice

Ju-hee narrates that outside inspections are rare and superficial.

> **DURING MY 3 YEARS HERE, I SAW A JOURNALIST COME HERE ONCE, AND THE POLICE TWICE.**

> **HOWEVER, THEY ALL FELL FOR THE ACT… AND WENT BACK AFTER TELLING US HOW THEY WANTED TO RAISE THEIR CHILDREN IN THIS ENVIRONMENT.**

> **BUT NOW… THERE WAS A SLIP UP.**

> **A SLIP UP DUE TO PERFECT MEMORIZATION.**

The child had memorized the phrase so completely that she used it on Han-rim despite Han-rim being a woman.

Ju-hee watches Han-rim with desperation.

> **I BEG YOU**

> **PLEASE REALISE**

> **THAT THIS IS ALL JUST AN ACT.**

Han-rim begins noticing discrepancies herself. The child identified as **Saint Seo-yool** has limited speech ability.

> **AND COMPARED TO THE OTHERS, SAINT SEO YOOL’S SPEAKING ABILITY IS QUITE…**

Han-rim scans the field.

> **NOW THAT I TAKE A LOOK AT IT, SOMETHING’S NOT RIGHT.**

Students of sharply different ages sit together doing the same elementary worksheets.

> **STUDENTS OF DIFFERENT AGES ARE LEARNING THE SAME SUBJECT.**

Other children wave insect nets in the grass.

> **THEY MAY SEEM LIKE THEY’RE HAVING FUN**

> **BUT THEY’RE SIMPLY FLAILING AROUND THEIR NETS WITHOUT ANY INTENTION TO CATCH BUGS…**

> **AND EARLIER, WHEN THIS CHILD SLIPPED UP…**

Han-rim’s conclusion becomes firm:

> **THIS IS ALL AN ACT!**

> **AN ACT CREATED TO FOOL THE OUTSIDERS!**

> **THEN, I SHALL TAKE MY LEAVE.**

> **MAYBE IF THERE WAS AN INSIDER…**

> **THIS IS ALL JUST AN ACT.**

## Images 12–13 — Ju-hee speaks publicly

Ju-hee steps forward and grabs Han-rim before she can leave.

Her internal resolve appears first:

> **HELP WON’T COME UNLESS I ASK FOR IT!**

> **THE ONES WHO DESERVE HELP…**

> **ARE THE ONES WHO CAN ASK FOR IT!!**

Then she shouts so that Han-rim, the pastor, the children, and the adult believers can all hear.

> **WE DON’T EVEN RECEIVE PROPER EDUCATION!**

> **WE’RE ALL BEING EXPLOITED BY THESE DIRTY BASTARDS!**

A flashback shows children and adults doing hard agricultural labor.

> **WORKING THE ENTIRE DAY!**

> **AND IF WE DON’T WORK WELL, WE ARE STARVED AND BEATEN!**

A darker image shows a victim bound or collapsed in a locked room.

> **IF WE SAY WE WANT TO LEAVE…**

> **WE’RE LOCKED AWAY AND TORTURED FOR DAYS!**

Ju-hee points to the flower-holding child.

> **SEO YOOL OVER THERE IS 9 YEARS OLD.**

> **BUT SHE DOESN’T KNOW HOW TO ADD OR SUBTRACT!**

> **WE WANT TO GO TO SCHOOL TOO!!**

Han-rim’s face changes from suspicion to certainty. She turns toward the pastor.

> **PASTOR…**

## Images 14–15 — The pastor tries to erase the accusation

Han-rim asks him directly:

> **WHAT THIS CHILD SAID, IS IT…**

> **ALL TRUE?**

The pastor smiles with controlled disappointment.

> **THIS IS QUITE UNFORTUNATE…**

He approaches Ju-hee and reframes her testimony as adolescent instability.

> **SAINT JU HEE IS GOING THROUGH PUBERTY RIGHT NOW, SO SHE IS QUITE UNSTABLE.**

> **BUT TO THINK IT WAS TO THIS EXTENT…**

He puts both hands on her shoulders.

> **SAINT JU HEE, WHAT DID I TELL YOU YESTERDAY?**

His public gentleness disappears as he leans close. His eyes glow yellow behind his glasses.

> **DID I NOT TELL YOU TO NOT THINK ABOUT THE HARD STUFF?**

He whispers the real threat while an image of Ju-hyeok appears in gray behind her.

> **THINK OF YOUR BROTHER, YOU BITCH!**

Ju-hee is crying and terrified, but she no longer retreats.

Ju-hee shoves away from him.

> **AND THERE’S ONE MORE THING!**

She points directly at the pastor.

> **YOU CALL ME OVER EVERY NIGHT…**

> **AND GROPE MY BODY, YOU FUCKING PERVERT!**

> **YOU ARE THE DEVIL!**

> **YOU MONEY CRAZED BASTARD!**

The pastor adjusts his glasses and gives an incredulous laugh.

> **HAHA… ME? THE DEVIL?**

His expression snaps into rage.

> **ME?!!!**

He slaps Ju-hee across the face, then strikes her again and again.

> **SAY THAT AGAIN!**

> **DEVIL…? DEVIL?!!!**

> **YOU DENSE FUCKER!**

Han-rim answers:

> **YEAH, HOW DARE YOU HIT A STUDENT…**

> **IN FRONT OF THE AGENCY?**

The pastor screams to the believers:

Men pour from the buildings carrying sticks, poles, tools, and makeshift weapons.

> **THE DEVIL HAS ARRIVED TO CORRUPT US ALL!**

The crowd gathers around Han-rim, Ju-hee, and the fallen pastor.

> **LOOK CLOSELY, THAT IS A WITCH!**

> **THE RED WITCH WHO HAS COME TO CORRUPT US ALL!**

Han-rim covers her mouth and laughs.

> **PFFT! WITCH?**

> **KYAHAHAHAHA!**

The cultists are confused and offended.

> **A WITCH? A WITCH YOU SAY?! KYAHAHAHA…!**

> **THE WITCH DARES TO LAUGH…?**

Several men rush her with hammers and clubs. Han-rim strikes two attackers in the face almost simultaneously, sending blood across the panel.

> **WOW~ I DIDN’T THINK I’D HEAR THAT IN KOREA TOO.**

She remembers her previous field work, where enemies had given her a supernatural nickname.

> **BACK WHEN I USED TO WORK ON THE FIELD…**

She takes two metal hammers from the attackers and holds one in each hand.

> **THE CODENAME MY ENEMIES GAVE ME WAS…**

A monstrous witch-like silhouette rises behind her.

> **BABA YAGA*!**

A footnote reads:

> **BABA YAGA — A FEARSOME WITCH FEATURED IN THE SLAVIC [FOLKLORE].**

The chapter ends with Han-rim smiling toward the armed crowd, no longer trying to leave quietly.

# End of Chapter 105

## Chapter continuity summary

Chapter 105 confirms that the alternative school is a carefully rehearsed fraud. The children are beaten into performing happiness, trained to imitate classroom behavior, and used to manipulate police and journalists. Han-rim notices the inconsistencies, but Ju-hee’s decision to testify converts suspicion into actionable evidence.

Ju-hee’s accusation also widens the case from educational neglect and forced labor to sexual abuse. When the pastor attacks her in front of the Teachers’ Rights Agency, Han-rim intervenes immediately. The cult answers with a manufactured “holy war,” and Han-rim accepts their language on her own terms: the “Red Witch” is revealed as the field operative once called **Baba Yaga**.
