> **THE DAY AFTER JANG HAERAN WAS ATTACKED**

Instead, Dabin created an entire online channel and began releasing evidence and witness accounts.

Multiple former victims started speaking publicly.

The channel grew beyond the agency’s original plan.

## Strips 003–004 — Five witnesses and a collapsing company

Seoyoung is physically ill from panic and anger.

A news report states that a fifth witness has testified on the “Ok Seoyoung’s Classmate” channel.

That witness attended Seoyoung’s elementary school and says Seoyoung bullied her severely. She also claims Seoyoung ordered or encouraged the later bullying of Yunju after Yunju transferred schools.

Within one week, five former victims or witnesses have spoken.

Ok Foods’ share price has fallen sharply—approximately thirty-five percent over the preceding month.

The police reopen the recent assault case against Seoyoung because new testimony supports Haeran’s account.

Reporters crowd around Seoyoung:

> **DID YOU REALLY INSTIGATE THE ASSAULT ON JANG HAERAN-SSI?!**

> **NEW TESTIMONIES ARE COMING UP ONLINE ALMOST DAILY. WHAT IS YOUR STANCE?!**

> **DON’T YOU PLAN TO APOLOGIZE TO THE VICTIMS?**

Seoyoung cannot understand why people remain interested in conduct she considers finished.

> **WHY ARE THERE SO MANY PEOPLE INTERESTED IN THE FUCKING PAST?**

> **IT’S ALL OVER NOW!!**

She argues that she is no longer bullying anyone and asks why she should suffer as an adult for things done in her “immature youth.”

Her father suddenly appears.

> **WHAT ARE YOU DOING?**

> **JUST THROW ON SOME CLOTHES AND COME OUT.**

He says they need to go somewhere.

## Strips 005–006 — Her father chooses Ok Foods

Seoyoung asks whether her father is taking her to a lawyer.

He says no.

They will visit the people making online statements one by one.

She will kneel, apologize, and then go to the police station to confess that she bullied them.

Seoyoung panics.

> **IF I DO THAT, I’LL GO STRAIGHT TO PRISON!**

> **I’M YOUR DAUGHTER!**

She tells him to protect his only child.

Her father answers with a grotesque version of parental favoritism.

If a parent has several children, he says, it is natural to prefer one.

He has finally realized which “child” he loves most:

> **IS OK FOODS.**

He says he will never forgive anyone who harms the company—not a nagging wife and not a daughter with a terrible personality.

Seoyoung is expelled from the family home and left outside in heavy rain.

> **I’M OK SEOYOUNG!**

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The accomplices face a race to confess

Daniel uses the uncertainty among Seoyoung, Dabin, and Yuni to make silence more dangerous than testimony.

## Public testimony exceeds the agency’s original plan

Dabin’s betrayal produces a channel where former victims can corroborate one another, transforming isolated memories into a visible pattern.

## Seoyoung’s father does not discover morality

He orders apologies only because Seoyoung threatens Ok Foods. The company replaces family as the object he protects.

## Seoyoung approaches empathy and retreats

Her brief question about whether the victims felt equally alone is immediately smothered by contempt.

## The chemical attack is premeditated

The red container is not carried by Yunju’s mother originally. Seoyoung brings it while planning retaliation, and Yunju’s mother intercepts her before she reaches Haeran.
