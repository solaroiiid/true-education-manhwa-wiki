The panels shift into fragments of Sumi’s handwritten account and memories.

> “A DISCRIMINATOR—”

Children point, crowd her, and repeat the label. Sumi crouches beside classroom storage, clutching her head.

# Chapter-level observations

### 1. Sumi’s punishment is collective

The shirt or sketchbook does more than shame her privately. It teaches every classmate to treat the teacher’s label as social permission to exclude her.

### 2. Yang’s ideology and conduct conflict

She condemns gender and social prejudice while insulting a divorced father and punishing a child for dissent. The contradiction is not incidental; it demonstrates that moral language can conceal ordinary cruelty.

### 3. Na’s deception is diagnostic

Na does not immediately accuse Yang. He flatters her expertise and lets her voluntarily display the full method. Her pride supplies the evidence.

### 4. Lim prevents the parent from being trapped

Mr. Shin’s anger is justified, but striking a protected teacher would shift the legal focus onto him. Na restrains him, and Lim assumes responsibility for the confrontation.
