## Strip 001 — Na demands the branch ledger

Na interrogates Wonsul inside the captured safe house.

He asks for:

- the customer list,
- the loan ledger,
- and all remaining assets.

Wonsul proposes a deal instead.

If Na releases him, Wonsul will provide the location of every G.Y. headquarters and the personal information of senior executives.

He says Na is targeting the entire organization, not merely one branch.

Na answers that he does not care about loyalty among criminals.

He would personally like to kill the organization’s current second-ranked figure.

A few hours earlier, he might have accepted Wonsul’s offer.

Then Sera reminded him of the agency’s most important duty.

Na punches him.

> **NOW, I’LL ASK AGAIN.**

> **WHERE IS THE CUSTOMER LEDGER AND THE REMAINING ASSETS?**

## Strip 003 — Wonsul offers to guide them personally

Wonsul finally understands what the team wants.

He asks whether they expect him to travel to headquarters and retrieve the missing money.

Na says they are attempting both objectives at once:

- recover victim assets,
- and follow the connection into G.Y.

Wonsul agrees to guide them.

He says they will not release him anyway, and the team would not trust a verbal location.

Na remains suspicious of the sudden cooperation.

The next day, the inspectors arrive at a luxury high-rise in Seoul.

Wonsul identifies the penthouse as the residence of:

> **JI YUN-GYEOL**

He describes Yun-gyeol as G.Y.’s number-two figure beneath the imprisoned Jo Gyuchul.

Na reacts because he has encountered the name before.

# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Asset recovery replaces revenge as the immediate priority

Na refuses Wonsul’s offer to sacrifice other G.Y. members because the victims’ money must be found first.

## The ledger quantifies the branch’s harm

The sports car, safe house, luxury goods, and cash represent only part of what was taken from 186 people.

## Wonsul cooperates for selfish reasons

He wants Ji Yun-gyeol dragged down with him and believes the headquarters can become a weapon against both rivals and inspectors.

## Yun-gyeol has already abandoned the cell structure

He gathers branch chiefs not to protect them, but to eliminate competing leadership.

## The explosion targets both sides

The trap attempts to kill the TRPA and purge inconvenient G.Y. executives simultaneously.
