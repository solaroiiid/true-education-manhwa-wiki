# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The campaign attacks credibility and privacy simultaneously

Vehicle damage makes the agency look weak, while sexualized manipulated images make individual inspectors objects of ridicule.

## Sera’s hidden identity creates both protection and bait

The perpetrators cannot target her through public records, so they plant a camera inside the agency and attack her directly.

## Na’s inaction is tactical

He is not dismissing the danger. He wants anonymous online participants to move from remote harassment into traceable physical crimes.

## The attackers are motivated by money and performance

They discuss angles, impact, prize money, and footage rather than political grievance.

## The revealed face does not immediately solve the case

The next chapter shows that none of the inspectors recognizes the young woman, meaning she is an intermediary rather than an old agency target.
