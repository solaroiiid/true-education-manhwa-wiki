# Chapter-level continuity notes

## The bullied girl was never a simple helpless target

From the beginning, witnesses sensed something unusual about her. She endured, but not with the blank resignation expected of a typical victim.

## Money was only the surface excuse

The real conflict concerns family history, inherited status, and the terror of losing social superiority.

## The aggressors were protecting hierarchy

They bullied and extorted not merely for cash but to suppress a truth that threatened the prestige of multiple families.

## The ending radically changes the case

What had looked like a completed tragedy is reopened by the victim’s own appearance. Her survival means the official story is incomplete, and the cycle she names may already have been intentionally broken by her.
