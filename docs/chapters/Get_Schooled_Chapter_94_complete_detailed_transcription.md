# The gambling system is shown openly

The story cuts to students staring at an illegal gambling interface.

A high-ranking gang member calls them idiots for playing without understanding the structure.

He explains the business model in three stages:

1. Let the player win money.
2. Make the player lose the money.
3. Offer credit or loans and turn the player into a debtor.

The debt is then used to create “slaves” who continually bring money to the organization.

One student promises to repay within three days. The collectors order him to bring his parents’ bank account information the next morning.

Another debtor has already sold belongings and taken money from his parents. The gang wonders aloud whether he will eventually sell out his friends as well.

The organization sees debtors not merely as customers, but as recruiters, thieves, and disposable labor.

Once students cannot pay, the organization uses them for theft, recruitment, account access, and other crimes.

## 3. Adult gangsters serve a youth-directed network

The older men supply violence, but the distributor and president make strategic decisions.

## 4. Lee Jung-hyuk invites the agency deliberately

Rather than hiding from the investigation, he plans to use a debtor as bait and confront the inspectors.
