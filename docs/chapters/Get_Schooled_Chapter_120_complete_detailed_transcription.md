# Get Schooled — Chapter 120
## Complete Detailed Panel-by-Panel Transcription

### Scope

This transcription follows all **27 ordered reader images** in Chapter 120. It records Sera’s undercover identification of Lee Chiho, his juvenile record, the class-wide extortion system, and the rooftop confrontation.

---

## Sequence 1 — Selecting a suspect

Agency staff provide attendance lists for every class. Because the known victims come from different grades and three are second-year students, Sera’s supervisor initially proposes starting with the second year.

Sera points instead to **first-year Class 2** and identifies **Lee Chiho**. When asked for her grounds, she proudly answers:

> **THE MOST SCIENTIFIC GROUNDS IN THE WORLD!**
>
> **THAT IS PHYSIOGNOMY!**

Her supervisor finds the method absurd but agrees to begin there. Sera also asks for a promise: while she is disguised as a student, the supervisor must not intervene regardless of what happens. She laughs and calls it a joke, but the request becomes important later.

---

## Sequence 2 — Lee Chiho’s record

The agency obtains a police background check. Chiho is eighteen but should have been a third-year student; repeated juvenile sanctions delayed his schooling.

The chapter presents his record in stark dossier panels:

- In first-year middle school, he stole a car, sped, and struck a pedestrian who required four months to recover. He received probation.
- In second-year middle school, he attacked a senior with a bicycle chain after being disciplined. The victim suffered facial paralysis; Chiho was sent to juvenile detention.
- In third-year middle school, he assaulted a drunken man and stole roughly eight million won, receiving a longer juvenile sentence.
- In first-year high school, he became a key member of a runaway-teen group and received the highest juvenile punishment, including two years of detention.

The supervisor becomes alarmed that Sera selected a far more dangerous target than expected. Chiho is not merely an intimidating student; he has a history of serious violence and little fear of institutional punishment.

---

## Sequence 3 — Undercover in Class 2

Sera enters the classroom and meets the class president, **Lee Seeun**, who invites her into the class group chat. Chiho sits in the room gambling on his phone. After five consecutive losses, he demands money from the entire class so he can recover what he lost.

The group chat fills with identical transfers of **31,250 won**. Students send the money immediately and add cheerful messages as though the extortion were voluntary.

Sera is fascinated by the updated method.

> **WHOA, SO THIS IS HOW KIDS THESE DAYS EXTORT MONEY? THEY’VE IMPROVED A LOT WITHIN ONE YEAR, HAVEN’T THEY?**

She notices that only fourteen payments arrived, excluding herself and the class president. Chiho also notices and erupts, demanding to know which students have not sent money.

The supervisor quietly says they have already collected enough evidence and orders Sera to back down for the day.

Sera refuses.

---

## Sequence 4 — Sera summons Chiho

Sera sends Chiho a message to come to the rooftop immediately, identifies herself as the person refusing payment, and insults him for extorting classmates to fund gambling.

> **HOW DO YOU FUCKING DARE TO EXTORT US FOR YOUR GAMBLING MONEY?**
>
> **IF YOU NEED MONEY THAT BADLY, WHY DON’T YOU TAKE A DAILY INSTALLMENT LOAN?**

The provocation is intentionally excessive. Sera wants Chiho to reveal himself in front of witnesses rather than allow the agency to remove him quietly on financial evidence alone.

The class president follows and begs Sera to stop. She promises Chiho that Sera is ignorant and can be taught, revealing how accustomed the class has become to negotiating with him.

Chiho blames the class president for failing to “teach” the newcomer properly. Sera responds with equal fury.

---

## Sequence 5 — The rooftop attack

Chiho arrives wearing metal knuckles. Sera’s supervisor finally reveals herself and warns that he does not take it easy on women. She says she will formally enter as an investigator the following day and orders Sera to retreat.

Sera reminds her of the promise not to intervene while she is undercover.

Chiho laughs at the prospect of hitting a girl and launches a full-powered punch at Sera’s face. The page uses large speed lines and heavy Korean impact lettering. Witnesses believe the knuckles connect cleanly and imagine facial fracture or brain hemorrhage.

The chapter cuts off at the apparent impact, leaving Sera’s condition unresolved.

---

## End-of-chapter continuity note

Chapter 120 contrasts evidence collection with Sera’s preferred form of discipline. The group-chat transfers already establish extortion, but she wants the frightened class to see Chiho’s invincibility broken. Her insistence on accepting the rooftop confrontation creates the cliffhanger continued in Chapter 121.

## Additional visual and text details

- Chiho’s dossier uses official portrait boxes and punishment numbers, visually contrasting the casual way he gambles in class with the severity of his history.
- The group-chat sequence shows near-identical transfers arriving within seconds. Students add emojis and friendly language, demonstrating how coercion is disguised as voluntary participation.
- Sera counts the messages rather than merely refusing payment; she identifies the class president as the other student who has not paid and therefore as a likely intermediary/victim.
- The class president’s pleading posture reveals her role: she survives by managing newcomers and preventing Chiho from becoming angry.
- On the rooftop, Chiho’s metal knuckles are shown in close-up before the swing. The witnesses’ imagined medical consequences heighten the impact, while Sera’s insistence that her supervisor stay back reveals the fight is part of the investigation rather than an uncontrolled quarrel.
