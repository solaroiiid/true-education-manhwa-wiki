# Chapter-level continuity notes

## Prescription abuse is shown as a system, not a single bad choice

The chapter connects hospital overdoses, parental pressure, doctors being coerced into prescriptions, online resale, and exam-season demand. Hyunmin’s dependence is therefore not treated as an isolated moral failure.

## Hyunmin’s mother confuses her own status with his welfare

Her fantasy is not primarily that Hyunmin will enjoy medicine or find a meaningful career. She wants to be known as “a doctor’s mother.” His achievement is imagined as compensation for her ordinary life, making his health disposable when it conflicts with the exam schedule.

## The “study drug” becomes chemically and psychologically compulsory

Hyunmin no longer believes he can read or prepare without medication. The chapter depicts craving, escalation, secrecy, overdose, hallucination-like behavior, and self-injury—far beyond the harmless productivity aid advertised online.

## Na’s intervention is physically extreme because the household is sealed

The mother refuses to open the door and plans to keep Hyunmin hidden until the exam. Na’s rope descent and window entry are framed as the only way to reach a student in immediate danger.

## The chapter ends before the rescue is resolved

Hyunmin is in an acute crisis when the chapter closes. Na and Hanrim have reached the apartment, but the outcome and the mother’s accountability are left for the next installment.
